{{Lang|'''Fuli'''|zh=浮黎|zh_rm=Fúlí}} is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Remembrance]].

==Description==
{{Transclude|Remembrance|Description}}

==Profile==
As the Aeon representing the Path of Remembrance, Fuli records happenings of the universe to prepare for the rebirth after the destruction.

No one knows of Fuli’s birth or origins. Although THEY are present in almost every significant event of the universe, THEY rarely manifest in front of mortals.

===Appearance===
Fuli manifests as a humanoid figure carved out of a reflective crystal material wearing a ''{{w|mianguan}}''.

==Abilities==
===Eden of Blessed Insight===
{{Main|Eden of Blessed Insight}}
The Eden of Blessed Insight, also known as the Pure Land of Recollection, is the realm of Fuli. In the tales whispered among the Memokeepers, Fuli will blend the most luminous and lavish memories into the Eden of Blessed Insight, and they shall serve to reshape the entirety of the world after its annihilation.<ref>{{Ref/VO|Black Swan|Sharing: Pure Land}}</ref> This realm has also shown a capability to see the future.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Anti-Organic War (I)}}</ref>

===Dreamscape===
When both the paths of Remembrance and [[Enigmata]] intertwine, the place known as the [[Dreamscape]] comes into existence.<ref>{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Travel Encounters｜Dreamflux Reef}}</ref>

===Memokeepers===
{{Main|Garden of Recollection}}
Memokeepers from the Garden of Recollection are dedicated to preserving and sharing memories. Enlightened by their master Fuli, Memokeepers shed their mortal flesh and live on as [[memetic entities]]. With this unique gift from Fuli, they can freely traverse between worlds, unconstrained by physical limits.<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Garden of Recollection — Remembrance}}</ref>

===Pure Children of Anāsrava===
{{Main|Pure Children of Anāsrava}}
Various women from very different birthplaces, races, identities and life experiences, but all with an innate connection to the Path of Remembrance. They are regarded as special [[Emanator]]s of Remembrance who can potentially become Fuli at the end of time.<ref name="db" />

==History==
===Origins===
After interfacing with [[Nous]], [[The Herta|Herta]] uncovers the truth about Fuli. Fuli has not yet been born; THEY will only ascend at the end of time in order to fulfill THEIR divine duty of recreating the universe once it reaches its [[Finality]]. At the moment of ascension, THEIR memory stretches backward through time, becoming the "Fuli" known in the past. Fuli's crystalline form is merely an empty shell, a secret the Garden of Recollection has long concealed. In the present, it remains uncertain who will ultimately become Fuli, but THEY will nonetheless be born from among the [[Pure Children of Anāsrava]].<ref name="silver" /> Other known instances of Fuli observed throughout the cosmos are merely false avatars, created as a consequence of the choice every Pure Child of Anāsrava must eventually make in their lives.<ref name="db" /><ref name="silver" />

Despite having not yet ascended, Fuli is not considered a fallen Aeon contrary to the rumors and claims propagated by the Cremators, who assert that Fuli fell and that the Garden of Recollection usurped THEIR divine authority over the Path of Remembrance. This is because masterless Paths do not exhibit tidal energy fluctuations, and fallen Aeons no longer gaze upon any Pathstrider &mdash; criteria that Fuli does not fulfill.<ref name="EoBL" />

===Swarm Disaster===
Some people claim that Fuli ascended during the conflict between [[Tayzzyronth]] and [[Oroboros]] in the [[Swarm Disaster]], in order to preserve the moment of the stars' demise. However, [[Herta]] considers this claim may have been fabricated by [[History Fictionologists]].<ref>Simulated Universe, Swarm Disaster, Chapters: [[Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster/Chapters#Remembrance I|Remembrance I]]</ref> Furthermore, [[Memory Bubble]]s existed before the Swarm Disaster,<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Gondola: Helping Gods!|1}}</ref> which suggests that the Garden of Recollection was already active at that time. This would require the existence of Fuli before the Swarm Disaster.

===Borderstar Trade War===
During [[Louis Fleming]]'s retirement speech, Fuli manifested in the crowd, and due to THEIR presence, the Candelagraphos was able to note three revelations coming from [[Nous]], [[Terminus]], and Mythus.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Inorganic: God's Three Revelations (I)}}</ref>

===Present===
When [[March 7th]] wished to learn about her past, the [[Messenger]] prevented her from doing so when she tried to go to [[Fu Xuan]] and use the [[Matrix of Prescience Ultima]] for help. According to the Messenger, THEY are interfering in order to protect her.<ref name="TotRec" />

===Amphoreus===
Traveling backwards through Amphoreus's history, the Pure Child of Anāsrava known as [[Cyrene]] created numerous false instances of Fuli in order to anchor causality and prevent the return of [[Irontomb]].<ref name="FP,LFToF">{{Ref/Mission|Fallen Petals, Leave Fading Traces of Fragrance}}</ref> The following events are all falsely attributed to Fuli:
* Fuli gazed upon [[Amphoreus]] when [[Cyrene]] and [[Phainon]] deleted "Time" from [[The Amphoreus Experiment]], causing every Cyrene that'll be born in the next cycle to gain the power to reset time.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Hero, Ignite That Primal Sun}}</ref>
* After gaining an audience with the [[Titan]] [[Oronyx]], the Titan realized that the [[Trailblazer]] could help them discover lost memories and find their "[[March 7th|Mother]]," taking an interest in them. During the process, Fuli gazed upon the Trailblazer while they were reliving their memories.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Night Veil, Shroud the Silent Past}}</ref> This was later revealed to have stabilized the Trailblazer's form, preventing them from dying in the meantime.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Spindle, Laboring to Weave the Tapestry of Time}}</ref>
* Trailblazer, accompanied by Dan Heng, enters the [["Universal Matrix" Great Tomb of the Nameless Titan|Great Tomb of the Nameless Titan]]. After restoring all the records preserved by Cyrene, they uncover the truth behind Fuli's involvement in the fate of Amphoreus. According to Evernight, Fuli had gazed upon Cyrene, planting a seed of false hope in her heart and leading her to believe that Amphoreus still had a chance to escape its destined ruin. In devotion to that hope, Cyrene recorded the memories of Amphoreus for Fuli, believing that THEY would one day save the world.<ref name="great tomb" />
* Gazing upon Cyrene following Irontomb's destruction of the universe, assisting her in recreating the entire universe with the help of the Trailblaze.<ref name="FP,LFToF" />

===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Remembrance</div>

...There are plenty of mysterious Aeons in the universe, and Fuli is particularly enigmatic. Nobody in the entire [[Genius Society]] and the [[IPC]] and the [[Intelligentsia Guild]] knows when THEY were even born. The Aeon of Remembrance had quietly appeared in the annals of the universe as if THEY were meant to be here, as if THEY had altered everyone's memory. Fuli rarely presents THEMSELF before mortals. According to video evidence, THEY visually look like a humanoid figure carved out of crystal, with indistinguishable facial features and wearing a diadem with a curtain of pearls covering THEIR face. The appearance of Fuli the Remembrance always signals an important event is about to happen, but I think maybe the cause and effect should be flipped &mdash; Perhaps Fuli only shows up to record that event in the universe's memories...?

===Dev Log2===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Remembrance</div>

...Fortunately, the one time that Fuli's arrival was recorded was the last public appearance of [[Louis Fleming]], the founder and lifetime honorable Board member of the Interastral Peace Corporation. Therefore, the IPC's archives preserved the complete recording of the Aeon's manifestation from the start to the end. Old Fleming was giving a speech at the time and declaring he was going to permanently retire from all official and unofficial events. Given how old he was, he was probably tired of living. But just as he was finishing the speech, there was suddenly a rainbow-colored light that threw everyone into disarray. Like a wraith that appeared in thin air, Fuli the Remembrance had condensed into being before Louis Fleming. The human representing wealth and the Aeon representing remembrance briefly locked eyes with each other. Maybe they exchanged some words, or maybe they didn't &mdash; The recording didn't manage to catch that. Fuli then disappeared, and Louis Fleming turned and walked off the stage, never to be seen again. Seriously, out of everyone who had faced an Aeon, that old man was the calmest I've seen. Even [[Screwllum]]'s gears squeaked when he saw Nous...

===Dev Log3 ===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Remembrance</div>

...Fuli, THEY are really hard to fathom. I doubt that the birth of the Remembrance is related to that widespread and vast fall of the stars. That final wailing and screams of countless lives before their demise shook me to the core. Alas, it was all the doing of [[Oroboros]] the Voracity! To be honest, everyone thought THEY were just a clumsy Leviathan, like how [[Qlipoth]] was thought to be an oversized statue. Who would have guessed that THEIR body has multiple forms? I suspect that there is a dimension unknown to humanity under the dark tides that gave birth to THEM. Oh, and THEIR abdomen, too &mdash; it is said that no species can resist the lantern-like "bait" on Oroboros's body, and that countless galaxies are already in THEIR stomach, with only the "memories" of those that perished being left behind thanks to Fuli...
