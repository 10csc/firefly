'''Oroboros''' is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Voracity]]. 

==Description==
{{Transclude|Voracity|Description}}

==Profile==
Oroboros was one of the oldest Aeons in the universe alongside [[Qlipoth]].<ref>Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log1|The Preservation: Qlipoth]]</ref> THEY were the last remaining Dusk Leviathan, a species that is alleged by the [[IPC]] to have disappeared when Qlipoth ascended.<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Oroboros the Voracity}}</ref><ref>NPC Dialogue: [[Welt/Dialogue|Welt]]</ref> Oroboros possessed a massive fanged tentacled neck with writhing flesh. THEIR body had multiple forms.<ref name="susdr1">{{Ref/Secret|Remembrance I}}</ref>

[[Herta]] speculates that Ena made a secret deal with Qlipoth regarding Oroboros.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Preservation I}}</ref>

==History==
At some unknown point in the past, [[Aha]] stole the concept of "Aftertaste" from the Path of Voracity, causing the genetic markers responsible for encoding gustatory organs, or taste buds, to vanish from all creations of Voracity, such as [[Void Song Whale]]s, turning them into biological black holes that consume without discrimination.<ref name="arcadia-mystery" />

During the [[Swarm Disaster]], when [[the Swarm]]'s propagation reached its peak, Oroboros devoured countless heirs of [[Tayzzyronth]], as well as numerous galaxies.<ref name="susdr1" /> It is said that no species can resist the lantern-like "bait" on THEIR stomach.<ref>Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons##Dev_Log3_2|The Remembrance: Fuli]]</ref> THEY also used THEIR "beautiful, alluring" singing voice to lure THEIR victims.<ref name="susdr1" /> Oroboros and Tayzzyronth eventually confronted each other in a war that lasted five Amber Eras. It affected two-thirds of the universe and caused countless planetary and star systems to perish.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Tragedy and Insects: The Dwindling of Stars|2}}</ref> As Oroboros devoured civilizations during THEIR war against Tayzzyronth, the memories of its victims crystallized and were preserved by Fuli.<ref name="susdr1" /><ref name="susdr2">{{Ref/Secret|Remembrance II}}</ref> Oroboros vanished during the final phase of the war along with [[Ena]], who had been watching THEM without THEIR knowledge.<ref name="susdr2" /><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Tragedy and Insects: The Dwindling of Stars|6}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Swarm Disaster: The End}}</ref>

THEY were supposed to be recorded in the [[List of Archenemies]] as one of the Authors of Calamity, but since THEY disappeared long before the [[Xianzhou Alliance]] could witness any of THEIR deeds, Oroboros was never added to that list.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Illusion of the End}}</ref> 

According to [[Ruan Mei]], Aha scattered the body of Oroboros across multiple worlds, including [[Planarcadia]]<ref name="arcadia" /> and [[Vonwacq]], and suppressed THEIR remains with the power of the Path of [[Abundance]] while siphoning the power of the Path of Voracity, calling it a "game." On [[Planarcadia]], this phenomenon is known as the [[Phantasmoon Games]].<ref name="arcadia-mystery" />

===Dev Log1===
Related Entry &mdash; The Voracity

...Oroboros the Voracity! THEY are reserved and uncommunicative, occasionally resulting in unintended consequences when attempting to speak &mdash; such as inadvertently consuming THEIR interlocutors. This often renders further discourse unnecessary. Had [[Louis Fleming]] encountered Oroboros the Voracity, he might have approached the Aeon with the intent to attract all wealth throughout the universe! Fortunately, Oroboros has vanished without a trace. Undoubtedly, many individuals are likely in search of THEIR whereabouts, and I am no exception. However, I choose not to indulge in tracking THEM down personally and instead will leave that task to [[Ruan Mei]] and [[Screwllum]]. What piques my curiosity more is the relationship between Oroboros and the other Dusk [[Leviathan]]s. As Oroboros stands alone as the sole known [[Aeon]] to have ascended from a Dusk Leviathan, it is conceivable that THEY may still have a reaction to the [[Shattered Star Bait]]...
