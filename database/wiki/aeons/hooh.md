===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Equilibrium</div>

...HooH the Equilibrium. THEY are one of the oldest [[Aeon]]s. The reason behind THEIR division into two halves remains beyond my comprehension. THEY separate concepts, splitting all beings into an interchangeable duality, thus fusing all "positives" and "negatives" in the universe into an equilibrial whole. THEY are always so symmetrical and so equal. If we were to rank those most sublime beings, only [[Oroboros|the Voracity]] and [[Long|the Permanence]] can stand toe-to-toe with the Equilibrium's antiquity... Oh, and also [[Ena|the Order]]. After Ena disappeared, the Equilibrium's duties have only grown greater. Then, how would HooH perceive [[Nanook]]?
