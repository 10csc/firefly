'''Mythus''' is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Enigmata]].

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Mythus the Enigmata|Aeons}}

==Profile==
Mythus strives to challenge the certainty of [[Nous]] the [[Erudition]] regardless of the consequences.<ref name="SU">Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log1 13|Mythus Dev Log1]]</ref> THEY conjured the Fog of Thought and the Rain of Sensation to reveal to the mortals the inexplicable truth through the four agents: Morph, Screen, Riddle, and Mirage.<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Mythus the Enigmata}}</ref>

===Appearance===
Mythus resembles a broken and torn jellyfish, akin to a deliberately destroyed version of the cognitive center of the human brain.<ref name="SU" /> THEY have a humanoid body surrounded by a staircase.

==History==
===Origin===
Mythus's origins are predictably unknown but according to [[Herta]], the [[Path]] Enigmata was born from the [[Remembrance]].<ref name="SU" /> It's possible that Herta was being more literal than she thought, as during the era of [[Astral Express]] Navigator [[Sam-3000]], the [[Nameless]] claimed to have seen Mythus manifesting in the reflection of the [[Eden of Blessed Insight]].<ref>Event Cosmodyssey, Report: [[Cosmodyssey/Report#Herald of the Sea of Stars|Herald of the Sea of Stars]]</ref> However, this may just be Mythus appearing and not a recount of THEIR birth. In [[Simulated Universe: Gold and Gears]], when the [[Trailblazer]] attempts to investigate the [[Eden of Blessed Insight]], realm of [[Fuli]], Mythus appears and pulls them away.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Anti-Organic War (I)}}</ref>

===Gold and Gears===
In [[Louis Fleming]]'s retirement speech, Mythus left a riddle with no question, but the answers are "blood" and "flower."<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Inorganic: God's Three Revelations (I)}}</ref> This prediction is about the subsequent murder of [[Rubert I]] in the hands of [[Polka Kakamond]].

During the [[Borderstar Trade War]], Mythus slightly shifted [[De Wayne]]'s usual position of Broadened Cognition.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Inorganic: Universe's Throne (I)}}</ref> After the Borderstar Trade War was over,  De Wayne lost his mind for some reasons unknown.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Broadened Cognition: Mental Spices (II)}}</ref> It is unknown if this is related to Mythus.

During the [[Mechanical Emperor's Wars|First Mechanical Emperor's War]], Mythus attempted to challenge the certainty of unchangeable moments calculated by Nous. When a group of [[Armed Archaeologists]] entered the [[Machine Empire]] to steal the Anti-Organic Equation, Mythus was present possibly to hinder their action, but these organic beings still accidentally sparked the Anti-Organic Equation and awakened [[Rubert I]].<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Judged as Inorganic: Liquid Gold (I)}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Anti-Organic War (II)}}</ref> Mythus intended but failed to shatter the concept of moments when [[Polka Kakamond]] was about to kill Rubert I.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Emperor Rubert (II)}}</ref>

===Memoria Leak===
At some point in the past, Mythus caused numerous breaches on the border between realspace and the [[Memory Zone]].<ref>{{Ref/Loading Screen|Memory Zone}}</ref> Enigmatic forces turn some [[Memory Bubble]]s into [[Dream Bubble]]s.<ref>{{Ref/Loading Screen|Dream Bubble}}</ref> It is suggested that THEY were responsible for the [[memoria]] leak in the [[Asdana]] system.

===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Enigmata</div>

...Mythus, undoubtedly the most mysterious among all the Aeons! This one is as destructive to the [[Simulated Universe]] as the simulated [[Aha]]. In the rare handful of authenticated accounts, Mythus' form is described as a broken and torn "jellyfish," akin to a deliberately destroyed version of... the "cognitive" center of the human brain. THEIR appearance is always accompanied by a fog of thoughts and rain of sensation. Symbolic objects dissolve like paint within THEIR shadow. History, knowledge, and language are soaked, drenched, and distorted in the deluge &mdash; Mythus wants to challenge the certainty of [[Nous|the Erudition]] regardless of the consequences &mdash; Is it truly worth it? To erase the past and distort the present all for the pursuit of an... uncertain future? Perhaps I can only hope to uncover more information about Mythus by digging into [[Remembrance]], the birthplace of Enigmata. Alternatively, I can try [[Finality]]...

==Trivia== 
* The Curio {{Item|"Jellyfish on the Staircase"|type=Curio}} resembles Mythus.
* "{{w|Thing-in-itself|Things-in-themselves}}," in the philosophy of {{w|Immanuel Kant}}, refer to {{w|noumena}}, objects as they truly exist independently of sensory perception. This contrasts with {{w|phenomena}}, which are objects as they are experienced through the senses. "Things-in-themselves" are unknowable to the human mind, apart from the phenomenal representations constructed by it.

===Etymology===
* Mythus refers to ''{{w|mythos}}'', a worldview-based traditional story or body of mythology.
