'''Aha''' is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Elation]].

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Aha the Elation|Aeons}}

==Profile==
Aha is one of the few Aeons closer to mortals, inspiring THEIR believers to delight in the joys of life. Although THEY don’t possess earth-shattering powers like [[Lan]] or [[Qlipoth]], Aha enjoys causing chaos through unpredictable means, making tiny changes and minor influences to living creatures.

===Appearance===
Aha takes a form of an obscured headless human figure holding circus-like props.

==History==
===Ascension Parable===
The Masked Fools share a parable about the Aeon: When Aha climbed to the highest branch of the [[Imaginary Tree]], THEY saw the void, the stars operating like machinery, and how meaning bows before nothingness. THEY continued looking until THEY saw a baby fall to the ground and cry. THEY burst into laughter so clear it tore through the universe and still reverberates to this day.<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Masked Fools &mdash; Elation}}</ref> Although the laughter that "tore through the universe" is widely regarded as a metaphor, the [[Masked Fools]] maintain that the event was literal and that the Echo of Primordial Laughter is the physical remnant left behind. The anomaly manifests as a spatial rift found within multiple Taverns operated by the Masked Fools throughout the cosmos. It is described as a motionless yet perpetually grinning fissure in reality, whose presence evokes both amusement and unease among those who encounter it. Despite its name, the rift produces no audible echo when shouted into, and objects cast into it vanish without a trace.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Medical Disputes on the Ship of Fools}}</ref>

At some unknown point in the past, Aha stole the concept of "Aftertaste" from the Path of [[Voracity]], causing the genetic markers responsible for encoding gustatory organs, or taste buds, to vanish from all creations of Voracity, such as [[Void Song Whale]]s, turning them into biological black holes that consume without discrimination. According to [[Ruan Mei]], the conflict between Aha and Oroboros stretches as far back to primordial times.<ref name="arcadia-mystery">{{Ref/Mission|Arcadia of Mystery and Terror}}</ref>

===Swarm Disaster===
The Masked Fools were drinking in their tavern when a blurred ripple appeared before them in a goblet which read: "Stop drinking, and go assist Aeons!". After three days, they reached the conclusion that it was from Aha, and set out on their journey. Generations passed as they finally realized that Aha was merely deceiving them and laughed at the futility of their actions.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Gondola: Helping Gods!|2}}</ref>

At the end of the Swarm Disaster, Aha, alongside [[HooH]], [[Qlipoth]], [[Akivili]], and [[Xipe]], came together and finally brought Tayzzyronth down and sealed THEM within amber.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Swarm Disaster: The End}}</ref>

At some point during or after the Swarm Disaster, Aha scattered the body of Oroboros across multiple worlds, including [[Planarcadia]] and [[Vonwacq]], and suppressed THEIR remains with the power of the Path of [[Abundance]] while siphoning the power of the Path of Voracity, calling it a "game." On [[Planarcadia]], this phenomenon is known as the [[Phantasmoon Games]].<ref name="arcadia-mystery" />

===Mechanical Wars===
As [[Rubert I]] fell into a deep slumber and the Machine Empire went to ruin, Aha laughed maniacally at the bleak scenery.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Anti-Organic War (II)}}</ref>

===Astral Express===
One day, Aha decided to pretend to be a [[Nameless]] for an entire year, just to blow half the train up to smithereens.<ref>Simulated Universe, The Elation: Aha: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log1 3|Dev Log1]]</ref>

===Unknowable Domain===
When [[Polka Kakamond]] invaded [[Simulated Universe: Unknowable Domain]], the simulated Aha helped [[Herta]] and broke Polka's Knowable Domain.<ref name="Waltz">{{Ref/Mission|Antinomic Waltz}}</ref>

===Planarcadia===
Aha is credited with creating the [[Phantasmoon]], the first imagenae on Planarcadia. Through the Phantasmoon, [[Wishpower]] is said to flow and manifest via the Path of Elation,<ref>{{Ref/Loading Screen|Phantasmoon}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Readable|Preface on the Basic Studies of Wishpower}}</ref> a phenomenon unique to the world.

Aha is also credited with inspiring the origins of the Phantasmoon Games. According to legend, eight Mourning Actors gathered beneath Panarch Elwood after being drawn by the spectacle of Aha's creation of the Phantasmoon. There, they shared their stories in the hope of earning an audience with Aha. Their collective performance would later be remembered as the first Phantasmoon Games, establishing a tradition that endured long afterward.<ref>[[Parlor Car]], Interactable: [[Parlor Car#IPC Broadcast #6|IPC Broadcast #6]]</ref><ref>{{Ref/HoYoLAB|I love it when Players say, "Huh?"}}</ref>

In Amber Era 2158, after all Supplicants of the Phantasmoon Games had been gathered, Aha, speaking through the [[Phantasmoon]], declared that the winner of this game would inherit the authority of Elation and become the true, permanent Aeon of Elation.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Real Holy Laughter!}}</ref>

===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Elation</div>
...The Elation, seriously? I don't feel elated when I think about Aha. All I feel is chaos. No other Aeon is as much of a headache as Aha. Aha has no sense of self-consciousness, is utterly unpredictable, does everything on a whim, and plays mortals and the universe like a fiddle. According to poor old [[Elias Salas]]' records, Aha once turned a [[Noblesse Worm]] into THEIR Emanator and granted the worm fathomless intelligence, just to see if Noblesse Worms could enter the [[Genius Society]] &mdash; The answer was no. For this pointless experiment, the Elation gave the entirety of THEIR Path's power to a worm that doesn't even have a fully developed brain, and then just discarded the creature once it had outlived its purpose. Without the Aeon's blessing, the worm died instantly and tragically. I don't know why Elias Salas recorded this in such great detail. Maybe he was shocked by this...?

===Dev Log2===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Elation</div>

...It'd be one thing if THEY only reveled in toying with mortals. The worst thing about this monster named Aha is that THEY won't even spare other Aeons as long as there's fun to be had. I obtained a large amount of valuable info from the [[Astral Express]]' data bank after I got to know the [[Astral Express Crew|Express crew]], including intel on close encounters between many Aeons such as Qlipoth, [[Terminus]], [[Ena]], and Xipe. Unsurprisingly, Aha was also involved in these interactions between Aeons, but in a way that far exceeded my imagination &mdash; Aha wanted to trick Akivili and the Nameless, so Aha shapeshifted into a human and laid low on Akivili's Express for more than a year, then finally got the opportunity and blew up half the Express and an entire planet. If I were Akivili, I'd have wanted to kill Aha. Pity I'm not an Aeon, and I can never figure out what THEY were thinking...
