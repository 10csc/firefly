==Profile==
Terminus travels against time from the future, having split THEIR divine corpus into a pack of black cats, empowering each cat with a part of THEIR divine power.<ref name="TWSB" /> According to [[Elegy]], a member of the [[Creed Exequy]], THEY appear at every moment of Finality, bringing prophecies of the future.<ref>NPC Dialogue: [[Elegy]]</ref> Terminus is described as being asleep, and the words heard are simply somniloquy, commonly known as sleep-talking.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Sand King: Tayzzyronth|1}}</ref> As THEIR words are merely murmurs, time has to be taken to decipher the information behind THEIR words, often driving some mad.<ref>{{Ref/Curio|Doomsday Compound Eye — Early Access Version}}</ref> There also appears to be a connection between Terminus and cuckoo clocks, with some people,<ref>{{Ref/Curio|Broken Cuckoo Clock}}</ref> including [[Louis Fleming]],<ref>{{Ref/Curio|IPC Cuckoo Clock}}</ref> building cuckoo clocks to better understand Terminus. 

The leader of the [[Stellaron Hunters]], [[Elio]], has an unknown connection to Terminus, with [[Constance]] regarding his existence as special. However, only the other members of the Stellaron Hunters know his true identity.<ref name="TWSB" />

Terminus has a prophecy called the [[Theory of Four Apocalypses]], which states that four Paths will push the cosmos towards its end: [[Destruction]], [[Harmony]], [[Nihility]], and a fourth Path that is yet to be determined.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Traveler, Find Truth Beyond the Illusion}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/YouTube|myriad_trailer = Exotale: Scene 8|date=Nov 11 2025}}</ref> 

When any one of the scenarios within the Theory of Four Apocalypses begins to unfold, Terminus shall ascend and begin to move forward back in time. At that moment, THEY'll manifest THEIR entire divinity into a clutter of black cats that travels across the universe, murmuring a prophecy that is destined to be fulfilled.<ref>{{Ref/Loading Screen|Terminus}}</ref>

==History==
During the [[Swarm Disaster]], when [[Ena]] was still looking over the universe, a dissonant interval was suddenly heard in the [[Beyond the Sky Choir]]'s singing, signalling that Ena and Terminus have brushed past each other.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Beyond the Sky Choir: Anomaly Archives|1}}</ref> This marked the end of Ena, as soon, THEY were assimilated into [[Xipe]] under the hand of [[HooH]].<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Beyond the Sky Choir: Anomaly Archives|3}}</ref>

After the events on Cyprogo, the blood of the people stained the library with blood. The [[Candelagraphos]] managed to discover Terminus' writing in one of the books, prophesying the start of the [[Mechanical Emperor's Wars#First Mechanical Emperor's War|First Mechanical Emperor's War]].<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Organic: Sanguine Condolences (I)}}</ref> After [[Louis Fleming]]'s retirement and the subsequent appearance of [[Fuli]], the Candelagraphos wrote down three paragraphs of the memories, revealing that Terminus had another prediction: That [[Polka Kakamond]] will bring about the end of the Mechanical Emperor's War.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Inorganic: God's Three Revelations (I)}}</ref>

After the events of [[Amphoreus]], with the aid of [[The Dahlia]], the [[Trailblazer]] used the power of one of Terminus' black cats to travel back in time and fulfill [[Firefly]]'s wish of seeing the fireworks.<ref name="TWSB" />

==Trivia==
* According to the Stellaron Hunters, when Terminus ascends to godhood once more, THEY will proclaim: "Now I am become Finality, lifting the wheel of time, the destroyer of worlds." This line likely references the {{w|Bhagavad Gita}}, specifically when {{w|Krishna}} reveals his divine form to {{w|Arjuna}} and declares: "Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds."
===Etymology===
* The word "terminus" has numerous meanings throughout history.
** The word "terminus" refers to a final point in spacetime and is likely where THEIR name originates from.
** Terminus can also mean "boundary" or "limit."
** The station at the end of a railway line is also referred to as a terminus.
** In Roman mythology, {{w|Terminus (god)|Terminus}} was the name of the god who protected boundary stones.
* {{zh|末王|rm=Mòwáng}} means "king of the end."
