==History==
===Origins===
Akivili originated from the planet [[Pegana]],<ref>{{Ref/Mission|An Invitation Without Proffer}}</ref> and created the [[Astral Express]] to transport THEMSELF and [[the Nameless]] across the galaxy. THEIR creation of the star rail network connected distant galaxies together and facilitated communication between them. The remnants of these star tracks are still used by the [[Interastral Peace Corporation]] and the [[Xianzhou Alliance]] today as safe routes.<ref name="glimpses" />

===Disappearance===
Akivili would disappear mysteriously one day during [[Dolly]]'s tenure as Navigator, leaving [[Isee]] and a group of loyal Nameless to carry on what THEY had started and continue THEIR exploration of the unknown. The Express eventually fell into [[Planarcadia]] during Asat Pramad's campaign to destroy the planet and all its passengers would die fighting against the [[Antimatter Legion]]. It laid dormant for an unknown period of time before Himeko discovered and repaired it.<ref>{{Ref/CS|Himeko|1}}</ref> According to [[Kafka]], [[Nous]] may be the only being who knows what happened to THEM.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|page=Wood Sprouts Anew, Fate Tilts Askew}}</ref> The [[Data Bank]] also alludes to an "accident" which abruptly ended Akivili's destiny, while [[Himeko]] refers to Akivili as deceased.<ref name="himeko" />

==Trivia==
* The [[Trailblaze Calendar]] used by the Astral Express is said to have come from Pegana, Akivili's home planet.<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Trailblaze Calendar (Calendar)}}</ref>
** The Trailblaze calendar system has each year split into twelve months, and each month is of different length. Apparently this calendar is still used in many of the planets that Akivili had once passed in their travels.
* The {{Item|Nameless's Memory Bubble}} is said to contain the dreams of Akivili, which can be purchased and replayed at [[Dr. Edward]]'s [[Dreamscape Sales Store]].
** After showing the Memory Bubble to [[Pom-Pom]] during the dialogue of Achievement ''[[The Worst Nameless Ever]]'', they admit that many things that have happened on this train aren't within the [[Trailblazer]]'s knowledge, and will talk about them when the time is right.
* In the main story of ''{{w|Honkai Impact 3rd}}'' Version 7.8, a Memokeeper from the [[Garden of Recollection]] claimed that Akivili has never reached the world of ''Honkai Impact 3rd''.

===Etymology===
* The name "Akivili" is likely derived from the Latin "aquili," meaning "of the north wind" or "of the eagle." Both etymologies are related to the Aeon's qualities, as the northern cardinal direction is important in navigation, and eagles are sharp-eyed and speedy.
