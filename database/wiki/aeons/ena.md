'''Ena''' is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Order]].

THEY were assimilated into [[Xipe]], when the latter ascended into Aeonhood, due to the [[Harmony]] overlapping with the concept of Order.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Wood Sprouts Anew, Fate Tilts Askew}}</ref> THEY were temporarily revived during the climax of [[Sunday]]'s actions on [[Penacony]].<ref name="Eight Day" />

==Profile==
Ena was one of the most ancient Aeons, characterized as a just and magnificent being. THEY thoroughly observed the actions of mortals to ensure the order of the universe,<ref>Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log3 1|Qlipoth: Dev Log3]]</ref> while displaying intolerance to any form of chaos or strife.<ref name="Pres 1">{{Ref/Secret|Preservation I}}</ref> Before THEIR fall, THEIR path had suppressed numerous calamities, such as the [[Swarm Disaster]].

THEIR voice is composed of syllables sequencing the rise and fall of civilizations in accordance with the Aeon's path. (Example: ♯1 - -·♭6 | ♭6 1· - - | - - - - ‖ ♬ Eme-Clent-J. I. Vadnbg. 1935-1942)<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Destruction II}}</ref> During the Swarm Disaster, Ena was assimilated by Xipe as the latter ascended.

===Appearance===
Ena takes the form of a giant eye observing and puppeteering a trunkless puppet veiled with tattered cloth and crowned with a golden halo. The halo is attached to a golden frame, framing the puppets face. Both the puppet itself and the cloth are white with golden accents. The puppet is holding and crystal-gazing into an orb containing the cosmos.

==History==
===Seven Days Myth===
After the Dusk Wars, darkness and chaos consumed the world. As such Ena ascended to become the Aeon of Order, marking the first day. THEY created a grand lyre with black and white keys &mdash; striking the white keys makes the sun rise, and the black keys makes the moon rise &mdash; creating the Calendar, which marked the second day.<ref name="EtRMC">{{Ref/Mission|Everything that Rises Must Converge}}</ref><ref name="SeptVO">"Harmonious Choir" The Great Septimus's Voice-Over: [["Harmonious Choir" The Great Septimus#Voice-Overs|Im Anfang war das Wort]]</ref>

THEY made stars into ink, creating Words, which marked the third day. THEY molded stardust into flowing rivers, the upstream for the righteous and the downstream for the unjust, creating Values, which marked the fourth day.<ref name="EtRMC" /><ref name="SeptVO" />

THEY used the rings of planets to establish Rules, which marked the fifth day. Through the existence of the Calendar, Words, Values, and Rules, humanity found their Meaning, which marked the sixth day.<ref name="EtRMC" /><ref name="SeptVO" />

Yet, all beings united and cast out Ena into the abyss of oblivion, representing Human Dignity, marking the seventh day.<ref name="EtRMC" /><ref name="SeptVO" />

===Swarm Disaster===
Witnessing the chaos caused by the war between [[Tayzzyronth]] and [[Oroboros]], Ena sought to put an end to this by seeking the "dreammaker that pulls and influences the will of transmutation", [[Qlipoth|Qlipoth the Preservation]].<ref name="Pres 1" /> An agreement was reached between the two Aeons, Qlipoth will put an end to Tayzzyronth, while Ena will help THEM against THEIR archenemy, Oroboros, by unknown means.<ref name="Pres 1" />

Although Ena was absorbed by Xipe while THEY ascended, preventing the deal from coming across,<ref name="Elat 2">{{Ref/Secret|Elation II}}</ref> Qlipoth fulfilled THEIR part nonetheless.<ref name="Pres 2">{{Ref/Secret|Preservation II}}</ref>

It is said that Ena has encountered Terminus of The Finality at some point in time.<ref name="BTSC AA">{{Ref/Secret|Beyond the Sky Choir: Anomaly Archives}}</ref>

===Present Day===
[[Gopher Wood]], alongside [[Sunday]], utilized Penacony's Stellaron and the [[Harmonic Strings|Harmonic String]] [[Dominicus]], the Wisher of the Harmonious Choir, in order to revive Ena in the Embryo of Philosophy and usurp Xipe. This plan failed as a result of the intervention of the Astral Express crew, [[Robin]], [[Black Swan]], [[Boothill]], and [[Acheron]].<ref name="Eight Day" />

===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Order</div>

...Ena the Order. I quite like THEM — I don't mean THEIR personality, since I can't get along with control freaks &mdash; but I can envision an ancient and romantic universe fashioned under THEIR unique "concept." The planets governed by Ena adhere staunchly to established rules, yet I must acknowledge that the various calamities in the cosmos were all hindered by Ena's guardianship, leading to extremely efficient development of civilization among diverse planets. Interestingly, however, civilizations all eventually hit rock bottom because of [[Order]]. The ancient planets that once fervently worshiped Order would shine briefly before their total collapse... Perhaps this is the "Path" of these planets. During the era when Ena the Order protected the cosmos, unequivocal splendor pervaded. Wherever THEIR divine traces appeared, it was always accompanied by the ethereal melodies of the [[Beyond the Sky Choir]]. These harmonious notes, meticulously arranged within a three-dimensional framework, form a holy hymn of Ena's own voice.

==Trivia==
===Etymology===
* Ena's name may derive from the Greek word ''ένα'' meaning "one."
** Ena may be inspired by the {{w|God in Abrahamic religions|Abrahamic God}}, "the Holy One," with angelic worshipers and whose holy day after the seven days of creation is Sunday.
* Ena's Chinese name "{{w|Chinese theology#Taiyi|{{zh|太一|rm=Tàiyī}}}}" is a name given to the supreme God of Heaven during the {{w|Han Dynasty}} in {{w|Chinese theology}}. It means "Great Oneness" or "Great Unity."
