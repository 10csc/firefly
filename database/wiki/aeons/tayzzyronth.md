'''Tayzzyronth''' is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Propagation]].

==Description==
{{Transclude|Propagation|Description}}

==Profile==
Tayzzyronth is an Aeon motivated by nothing but the need to propagate and spread across the universe.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Bounty Hunter: Crimson Cleansing Chronicle|2}}</ref> THEY were responsible for the rise of [[The Swarm]], and its eventual spread throughout the universe during the [[Swarm Disaster]]. 

===Appearance===
Tayzzyronth appears as a large insect in black, wine red, and other matching colors. THEY have six membranous wings, three on each side, with four tinted with white. Six sharp legs emerge from THEIR main body. On top of THEIR main body is a pale-skinned, faceless, puppet-like humanoid with two small wings on THEIR back. THEIR head is replaced with a pair of pink wings fading to dark blue on the tips. THEY appear to be holding a [[Juvenile Sting]].

When THEY appear in [[Simulated Universe]], a loud screech can be heard, accompanied by the fluttering of wings. In the distance, more cries can be heard.

==History==
===Ascension===
{{Main|Swarm Disaster}}
Tayzzyronth hailed from the planet [[Lepismat]] where THEY were the last of the [[Coleoptera]] after a war between the Elydichnan army and a group of "[[Bounty Hunters]]."<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Sand King: Tayzzyronth|7}}</ref> Feeling intense loneliness for THEIR species, Tayzzyronth ascended as the Aeon of Propagation,<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Sand King: Tayzzyronth|1}}</ref> splitting the [[Path]] of [[Permanence]] from then deceased Aeon [[Long]].<ref name="Wood Sprouts">{{Ref/Mission|Wood Sprouts Anew, Fate Tilts Askew}}</ref>

===War between Aeons and Swarm Disaster===
In 13XX [[AE]], Tayzzyronth and [[The Swarm]] began their spread across the universe.<ref>Honkai: Star Rail (September 13, 2023). [https://www.hoyolab.com/article/21593235  Everything we know about the Swarm Disaster so far]. HoYoLAB. Retrieved April 25, 2024.</ref> One day, the Aeon [[Oroboros]] appeared before THEM and this provoked a war between the two, which affected two-thirds of life in the universe.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Tragedy and Insects: The Dwindling of Stars|2}}</ref> Oroboros opened THEIR mouth and swallowed numerous heirs of Tayzzyronth along with countless worlds.<ref>Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster: [[Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster/Chapters#Remembrance_I|Remembrance I]]</ref>

While Oroboros and Tayzzyronth fought, the ancient Aeon [[Ena]] would send an invite to [[Qlipoth]] in order to settle the war.<ref>Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster: [[Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster/Chapters#Preservation_I|Preservation I]]</ref> While it remains unclear what exactly THEY planned together, Ena would later disappear,<ref name="the end">{{Ref/Mission|Swarm Disaster: The End}}</ref> assimilated by [[Xipe]].<ref name="Wood Sprouts" /><ref>Simulated Universe, Swarm Disaster: [[Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster/Chapters#Elation II|Elation II]]</ref> The forces of Qlipoth, [[HooH]], [[Aha]], [[Akivili]] and Xipe would team up and attempt to kill Tayzzyronth, and although Tayzzyronth attempted to give up THEIR ability to propagate in order to be spared, this didn't appease the Aeons.<ref>{{Ref/LC|On the Fall of an Aeon}}</ref> Qlipoth used THEIR hammer to dilute the Path of Propagation and stripped Tazyzzyronth of THEIR power.<ref>Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster: [[Simulated_Universe: Swarm Disaster/Chapters#Preservation_II|Preservation II]]</ref> While many sources will claim Tayzzyronth died in this moment, THEY and THEIR path live on, imprisoned in amber with the chance to return.<ref name="glimpses" /><ref name="the end" />

===Legacy=== 
While Tayzzyronth's Aeonhood was short-lived, the ruin THEY caused on the universe is comparable to that of [[Rubert I|Emperor Rubert]]. The [[Armed Archaeologists]] from the Intelligentsia Guild have provided evidence that stars seeded by the Tayzzyronth have irreparably become literal hellscapes, and their only end lies in obliteration by star destroyer arsenals to prevent future danger.<ref name="glimpses" /> When [[Adlivun]] was attacked by [[the Swarm]], it indirectly led to [[Nanook]]'s ascension into Aeonhood.<ref>Simulated Universe: Nanook [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log2 5|Dev Log 2]]</ref>

===Potential Reappearance===
According to [[Herta]], Tayzzyronth may eventually resurface in the future.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Swarm Disaster: Exchanging Secret Intel}}</ref> This is further expanded upon by [[Fu Xuan]] in her writings ''[[Glimpses into the Beyond]]'', wherein she recounts learning from a scholar that Tayzzyronth was sealed in amber. He showed her a piece of amber and claimed it was a part of the "Imperator Insectorum's Prison Cell" and the omniscia in Fu Xuan's forehead could not foresee the future of the item, corroborating the scholar's claims.<ref name="glimpses" />

After [[Jingliu]] and [[Luocha]] revealed that they had smuggled the [[Stellaron]] onto the [[Luofu]] (which was a feint), an investigation led by [[Yao Guang]] revealed that they had a Godslayer Protocol to end [[Yaoshi]] &mdash; Luocha's coffin actually contained a fragment of Tayzzyronth's divine body, which would serve as the final nail in the Plague Author's fate and doom.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|A Fugue From Beyond}}</ref>

===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Propagation</div>

...Tayzzyronth the Propagation! I don't know why [[Ruan Mei]] is so interested in THEM. Perhaps because THEY made such a ruckus with the [[Swarm Disaster]] THEY created. However, this creature is timid, slow-witted, and has no insight as an [[Aeon]] &mdash; THEIR mind is void of anything except reproduction, and even THEIR [[The Swarm|descendants]] are the same. In the days before the fall of Tayzzyronth, some had also attempted to breed the Swarm and make it think beyond mere propagation, but it seemed to have been a complete failure. They couldn't even put a shoe on themselves. Though, you have to hand it to Tayzzyronth. THEY went so far down the road of Propagation that just a little shiver could make the Swarm throughout the entire universe start a frantic, endless self-replication &mdash; seriously, why the overreaction? Since THEIR fall, there are still an endless number of the Swarm rampaging throughout the universe, and they are really annoying...

===Dev Log2===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Propagation</div>

...With the Propagation's intelligence, THEY probably didn't have a chance to realize how pitiful THEY were. Tayzzyronth fell quickly, just several [[Amber Era]]s after THEIR birth, leaving nothing behind in the universe except a [[The Swarm|Swarm]] as far as the eye can see. The only thing confirmed by the evidence is that there must have been something behind that cataclysmic massacre on [[Lepismat]], but I'm afraid that even the one behind the act never anticipated for Tayzzyronth to ascend to godhood by "chance." Or maybe the mastermind just didn't care. Now, what else but an Aeon could have played such a conniving role? Also, I made another discovery: It seems that, biologically, Tayzzyronth cannot be clearly classified into any of the genders present in sexually reproducing insect species. Does this discovery have any significance? It doesn't seem to. I'm just documenting it...
