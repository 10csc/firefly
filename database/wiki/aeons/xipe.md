'''Xipe''', titled the '''Great One''' by THEIR followers, is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Harmony]].

==Description==
{{Transclude|Harmony|Description}}

==Profile==
Xipe is the amalgamation of thousands of entities that preaches the joy of human harmony and unity hailing from multiple harmonious celestial worlds.<ref>{{Ref/Loading Screen|Xipe}}</ref> THEY absorbed [[Ena]] the [[Order]] when the former ascended into an Aeon due to THEIR colliding Paths nearing the end of the [[Swarm Disaster]].<ref>Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log1 5|Nanook Dev Log1]]</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Wood Sprouts Anew, Fate Tilts Askew}}</ref>

===Appearance===
Xipe presents as a serene, periwinkle-skinned humanoid with three faces &mdash; two on either side of THEIR head and one on the front. The faces are framed with lengthy white curtain bangs and are each adorned with a circlet. Xipe's extensive swathe of hair is colored white with a twinkling dark purple ombre, aloft a polychromatic flare reminiscent of a halo.

The substance of Xipe's body flays into jigsaw-like fragments, covering it in a form-fitting dress that is decorated with nebular expressions of the cosmos. The dress’ lower part flows past THEIR feet, with firm lattices in gold wrapping around the collar and torso.

==History==
===Ascension===
In 13XX [[AE]], [[Tayzzyronth]] and [[The Swarm]] began their spread across the universe, marking the beginning of the Swarm Disaster.<ref>{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Everything we know about the Swarm Disaster so far|date=Apr 25 2024}}</ref> It was during this time that Xipe absorbed the Aeon of Order, Ena.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Elation II}}</ref> Xipe would later assist [[Akivili]], [[Aha]], [[HooH]], and [[Qlipoth]] in sealing Tayzzyronth.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Swarm Disaster: The End}}</ref>

===The Family===
{{Main|The Family}}
Xipe and THEIR chosen ones formed a harmonious "Family" composed of members from various civilizations and worlds. They are the closest family there is and believe there should never be contradictions or noisy disputes. Only with eternal love and smiles will the universe become a harmonious whole. The Family frequently calls out to other worlds with their song, encouraging them to embrace Harmony. The Family will celebrate each new world that accepts the blessing of Xipe, and work even harder at praising the Aeon's kindness. The Family has never known any world that grew weary of the singing and voluntarily abandoned the Path of Harmony.<ref name="family">{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Astral Express Magazine - Penacony Info Bulletin "The Family"|date=Mar 19 2024}}</ref>

====Penacony Chapter====
[[Penacony]] is a chapter of The Family, its previous spokesperson being [[Sunday]].<ref name="family" /> Penacony, Land of the Dreams, is an [[The Reverie (Reality)|intergalactic hotel]], wherein the real Penacony is entered through one of the hotel's Dreampools. The Penacony of the Dreamscape is crafted through the combined efforts of Family members, Dreamchasers, and guests.<ref name="hotel">{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Travel Encounters - Penacony Travel Guide: Hotel|date=Mar 19 2024}}</ref>

As Sunday later revealed his true loyalty to the Order and put into action his plan of trapping everyone in a perfect dream, he was confronted by the [[Trailblazer]], [[Himeko]], [[March 7th]], and [[Firefly]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Beauty and Destruction}}</ref> When the Trailblazer received [[Falcon Amundsen]]'s hat from the [[Watchmaker]], the group was gazed at by Xipe,<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Then Wake to Weep}}</ref> however it was unclear to the group why Xipe had chosen to look at them.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Everything that Rises Must Converge}}</ref> Much later, the [[Intellitron]] [[Stefano]] commented that he could sense the Harmony inside the Trailblazer.<ref>NPC Dialogue: [[Stefano]]</ref>

==Abilities==
===Harmonic Strings===
{{Main|Harmonic Strings}}
The Harmonic Strings are the multiple embodiments of Xipe. They are the down-to-earth virtues that enable Harmony. Some argue that the Harmonic Strings are nothing short of [[Emanator]]s of Xipe, however they do not follow any specific mortal but are facets of Xipe that can assume any member of The Family when necessary. The names of the embodiments of Xipe are [[Aelenev]] the commander of the Eternal Centurion, [[Dominicus]] the wisher of the Harmonious Choir, [[Constantina]] the singer of the Panacoustic Theater, and [[Beatriz]] the merrymaker of the Blissfull Ball. Few have seen these manifestations outside The Family.<ref>{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Pom-Pom's Intel Emanator Special|date=Mar 19 2024}}</ref>

===Stellarons===
{{Main|Stellaron}}
Stellarons are mysterious objects that are vague, imperceptible matter which disrupt the flow of Imaginary Energy along the interstellar routes created by [[Trailblaze]] that are spread across the universe by Xipe the Harmony, as THEIR "Harmonic Cancer."<ref name="OitT">{{Ref/Mission|Omen in the Trees}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Cancer of All Worlds (Phenomenon)}}</ref> They are considered seeds of disaster which cause massive changes to civilizations and ecosystems, and result in the appearance of [[Fragmentum]].<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Fragmentum (Phenomenon)}}</ref> They respond to the desires of the world to which it is tethered. However, the cost of fulfilling those desires often brings great disaster to the world. There is no known way to destroy a Stellaron; currently, the most that can be done is contain its energy.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Silent Galaxy}}</ref>

===Dev Log 1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Harmony</div>

...Xipe the Harmony. Oh, the mother of all stars! THEY proclaimed harmonious hymns of celebration, and united all wills as one. The Family's songs spread to all corners of the world, and THEIR degree of understanding, support, and synergy for the will of all creatures was beyond imagination. Or perhaps, THEY were already on a plane beyond mortal minds. The ever-expanding Harmony was truly worthy of human longing. The universe encircles and decorates THEIR body like crushed diamonds, and civilization became a rainbow to be snatched in THEIR palm. I am filled with curiosity about how THEY swallowed up Order. The hymns of Xipe continue to spread and grow, occasionally overseen by ideology. In contrast, Ena's harmonic songs seems to align within a three-dimensional framework, akin to an emperor maintaining hierarchical order among all creatures. While there may be some overlap between THEIR Paths, the ancient Order is enormous in size, and swallowing THEM would prove far from effortless... Among the Aeons, there exist countless enigmas that surpass my own speculations.
