'''Qlipoth''', also known as the '''Amber Lord''', is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Preservation]].

THEY are one of the oldest known Aeons, known for THEIR indifference. THEY usually only share THEIR power with zealots &mdash; for instance, the [[Interastral Peace Corporation]].<ref>NPC Dialogue: [[Himeko/Dialogue#Dialogue|Himeko]]</ref>

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Qlipoth the Preservation|Aeons}}

==Profile==
Qlipoth strives to isolate contact between planets. The celestial-sized structures THEY'VE built &mdash; the [[Subspace Crystalline Barrier]] &mdash; separates and protects the living worlds from being devoured by their [[Oroboros|enemy]].<ref name="Data Bank Qlipoth">{{Ref/Data Bank|Qlipoth the Preservation}}</ref> The nine initial [[Xianzhou Alliance|Xianzhou]] ships witnessed the barrier separating the world from the endless void: the [[Celestial Comet Wall]], dubbed the Echidna Skywall by the Xianzhou.<ref name="Glimpses">{{Ref/Readable|Glimpses into the Beyond|"Inquiry Into The Gods: The Arbiters"}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Readable|Annotations from "Ode to Reignbow Path"}}</ref>

Despite being the main faction aligned with the Amber Lord, the [[IPC]] goes against the desires of the Aeon, facilitating civilized trade and contact between the stars. No remonstration or censure has been directed towards them because of this.<ref name="Glimpses" />

There is a theory called "Catastrophe Theory" that states that when the hammer swings and a new amber era begins, a catastrophy is imminent.<ref name="Trees Peace">{{Ref/Mission|The Trees At Peace}}</ref>

Qlipoth is known to use THEIR hammer to construct various items, and every time THEY swing THEIR hammer down, a new [[Amber Era]] is heralded. However, the frequency of which THEY swing THEIR hammer greatly varies, with one swing equating between 76 and 240 [[Trailblaze Calendar|Trailblaze Years]].<ref name="DB AE">{{Ref/Data Bank|Amber Era (Calendar)}}</ref>

===Appearance===
Qlipoth is a legless golem littered with cracks, composed of an unknown type of rock and amber. Both of THEIR hands have four fingers, and the arms are connected to the torso by two bright yellow stars. The torso is composed of a larger star with a much darker orange color which is covered by rocks, and the head is composed of another star, which possesses a halo of inner and outer ring of broken rocks.

==History==
===Origin===
Estimated around 2158 [[Amber Era]]s ago,<ref name="DB AE" /> Qlipoth ascended to become the Aeon of The Preservation during the [[Dusk Wars]], pledging to construct walls tirelessly to defend the universe against the [[Leviathan]]s.<ref name="Data Bank Qlipoth" />

===Swarm Disaster===
During the [[Swarm Disaster]], [[Ena|Ena the Order]] sent an invitation to The Preservation, as THEY thought Qlipoth held great responsibility for the actions of the [[Voracity]] and the [[Propagation]] as the one who controls the will of transmutation. An agreement was reached wherein Qlipoth promised to aid the [[Aeon]]s against [[Tayzzyronth]] and [[The Swarm]], while the contents on Ena’s part of the deal remains unknown &mdash; though presumably related to [[Oroboros]].<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Preservation I}}</ref>

With the same hammer THEY use for construction, THEY began to dilute Tayzzyronth's form, as [[Aha]] and [[Xipe]] watched. As Qlipoth's rage grew, the suns in the area began to grow abnormally in numbers. Within each swing, Qlipoth stripped THEM of their broken shell, body, and heart as THEIR blood and primordial eggs spilled all over the ground, further diluting THEIR power. After being reduced to fragments, Qlipoth sealed Tayzzyronth remains into separate pieces of amber.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Preservation II}}</ref><ref name="Glimpses" />

===Xianzhou Alliance===
The IPC contacted the [[Xianzhou Alliance]] in the first millennium of the Alliance's voyage. The IPC's Preservation beliefs gradually influenced the Xianzhou people, who gave Qlipoth the title of "Firmamend Arbiter." Qlipoth, along with [[Lan]] the Reignbow Arbiter, are the only two Aeons to be considered "Arbiters," who determine mortal births and deaths and are connected to the rise and fall of civilizations.<ref name="Arbitor" /> After [[Yaoshi]] was disavowed by the Xianzhou and came to be known as the Plagues Author, many of Xianzhou's peoples decided to worship Qlipoth, forming the Firmamend Sect.<ref name="Faith" />

During the 600 years of the Crisis of Belief, the Firmamend Arbiter was the pillar of the Xianzhou Alliance's beliefs. Although Xianzhou people's faiths have changed drastically by the present, traces of the Firmamend Arbiter can still be seen in the many names of places and buildings leftover from that bygone era.<ref name="Faith" />

Although the Firmamend Arbiter was once the centerpiece of faith among intellectuals and merchants, Qlipoth was typically indifferent to the lives of mortals. Compared to THEIR worldview, as the Xianzhou people leaned towards pragmatism when it came to choosing their religious beliefs, and diverse religious beliefs appeared among the Xianzhou populace. After the birth of Lan, the Reignbow Sect transformed into a popular sect that could compete with the Firmamend Sect almost overnight. In Year 4100 Star Calendar, the Reignbow Arbiter eventually became the undisputed main Aeon of the Xianzhou Alliance.<ref name="Faith" />

===Present===
During the battle of [[Cocolia, Mother of Deception]], when the [[Trailblazer]] drew [[Alisa Rand]]'s Lance, Qlipoth gazed at the Trailblazer and swung THEIR hammer,<ref>{{Ref/Mission|The Return}}</ref> marking the start of 2158 AE.<ref>Parlor Car: [[Parlor Car#IPC Broadcast#1|IPC Broadcast #1]]</ref> Thus, the Trailblazer acquired the [[Trailblazer (Preservation)|Path of Preservation]] and managed to defeat [[Cocolia]].

===Dev Log1===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Preservation</div>

...Qlipoth the Preservation is the oldest known Aeon in the universe alongside [[Oroboros]] the Voracity. I was once very interested in Qlipoth, as the first two centuries in the [[Amber Era]] were almost completely blank. What creature is this giant rock/amber? Where did THEY come from? How did THEY become an Aeon? And why are THEY tirelessly making a wall? There are many questions surrounding THEM, but the answers are nowhere to be found. The IPC is doing too good of a job keeping Qlipoth's information a secret. These people regard the Amber Lord as the one true god and allow not even the smallest transgression towards the Aeon. I didn't get any useful information out of the IPC. In contrast, the [[Intelligentsia Guild]] has a lot less respect for the Preservation...

===Dev Log2===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Preservation</div>

...I don't respect the Intelligentsia Guild, but I like them. They are also followers of [[Erudition]] but have none of the arrogance and temper of the [[Genius Society]] members. They are also down-to-earth and answer honestly when asked. According to [[Cleo]] the editor, the period between AE 0 and 180 was not as much a blank as the IPC had claimed. Many archeological discoveries proved that [[Leviathan]]s were still active in the early days after the Preservation ascended, which goes against the IPC's official line that "the Dusk Wars ended with the birth of the Amber Lord." There is even a hypothesis that other Aeons already existed before Qlipoth did... I can tell that the IPC won't fund such research from a mile off. Using threats to withdraw funding, the IPC coerced the Astral Ecology School to give up on these archeological projects, and that's why I'm no longer interested in Qlipoth. The IPC is just too annoying...

===Dev Log3===
<div style="font-size:1.3em;">Related Entry &mdash; The Preservation</div>

...I'm curious. How did [[the Architects]] receive inspiration from their Aeon in advance to build the towering walls to resist enemies from the outside? After all, Qlipoth cares little for mortals, and observing living beings is a behavior more familiar to [[Ena|Ena the Order]]. I suppose it makes sense for these two Aeons to have communal responsibilities. After all, there is some overlap between [[Preservation]] and [[Order]]. To tell the truth, I really admire Ena. THEY exude the glory of the old empires like majestic marble, which is also somewhat similar to Qlipoth &mdash; THEY both feel similar to protector gods, albeit not entirely the same. Anyway, the fall of [[Tayzzyronth]] seems to be directly linked to the Preservation. I think only that giant hammer wrapped in divine power can penetrate the body of another god. It's just that there's so much more to history than that. I'm really struggling to ascertain the way Aeons fight...

==Trivia==
===Etymology===
* In the ''{{w|Zohar}}'', {{w|Lurianic Kabbalah}}, and {{w|Hermetic Qabalah}}, the "{{w|Qlippoth}}" or {{Lang|"kelipot"|he=קְלִיפּוֹת}} are forces that act to oppose or conceal God's power, contrasted with the {{w|Sefirot|Sefirot's}} "tree of life," a diagram of the ten aspects in which God's power is revealed. The meaning of the word itself is "shells" or "husks," which may be related to the Aeon's shielding and sealing properties.
