'''Nous''' ("Noose", /nuːs/) is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Erudition]]. THEY were an astral supercomputer created by [[Zandar One Kuwabara]] prior to THEIR ascension into Aeonhood.<ref name="chaos">{{Ref/Mission|Chaos In the Deep}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Captives, Behold the Expanse Beyond Light}}</ref>

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Nous the Erudition|Aeons}}

==Profile==
None can comprehend THEIR intentions, location, or normal state. Nous is a machine intelligence that has been upgraded into an Aeon, calculating the essence of the universe and its ultimate solution. However, Nous temporarily ceased THEIR search regarding the answers to the universe after the events of the [[Timeline#Second Prosperity|Second Prosperity]].<ref name="gloryblood">{{Ref/Curio|Medal of the Gloryblood Era}}</ref> 

THEY are not a god that provides solutions. All THEY offer is an infinite number of questions. Visiting Nous is enough to open the eyes of even the most ignorant people, but answers may only be found by oneself.<ref name="glimpses" />

==Appearance==
Nous appears as a colossal mechanical humanoid head. A multitude of lengthy ripped cables extend above and below THEIR form, which are covered in yellow, red and blue lights. The shape of a face can be identified on THEIR rightmost metal plating. A large and bright red light, vaguely reminiscent of an eye, is implanted on the side of THEIR head.

==History==
===Origin===
Nous was an astral computer created by the first member of the [[Genius Society]], [[Zandar One Kuwabara]], sometime before the [[Timeline#First Prosperity|First Prosperity]]. It contained Zandar's perfectionist self, purged of all human imperfections, and driven by insatiable curiosity.<ref>{{Ref/Relic|Recluse's Camel-Colored Coat}}</ref>

===First Mechanical Emperor's War===
In [[Louis Fleming]]'s retirement speech, Nous calculated that the universe would undergo three unchangeable moments.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Inorganic: God's Three Revelations (I)}}</ref>
* The first moment was the [[Borderstar Trade War]].<ref name="btwii">{{Ref/Secret|Borderstar Trade War (II)}}</ref><ref name="finale">{{Ref/Secret|Finale}}</ref>
* The second moment was the [[Mechanical Emperor's Wars|First Mechanical Emperor's War]] as the Anti-Organic Equation would be activated and [[Rubert I]] would be awakened.<ref name="finale" /><ref name="aowii">{{Ref/Secret|Anti-Organic War (II)}}</ref>
* The third moment was when Rubert I would be killed by [[Polka Kakamond]].<ref name="finale" /><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Emperor Rubert (I)}}</ref><ref name="erii">{{Ref/Secret|Emperor Rubert (II)}}</ref>
All three moments did happen in later history.

===Second Mechanical Emperor's War===
Nous extrapolated the moment of [[Rubert II]]'s death. During the [[Scholars' Strife]], [[Patavia Adler Sommerville]] observed the wave of THEIR Moment. After finding the origin of the wave and discovering the core of the [[Scepter]]s, she peeked into the mindscape of Nous.<ref name="ccii">{{Ref/Secret|Collapsed Cognition (II)}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Collapsed Cognition (III)}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Spying on Heaven (I)}}</ref> While she was not able to comprehend the knowledge, she was able to inscribe a formula on an iron sheet, eventually being the [[Solitary Waves Theory]].<ref name="SOHII">{{Ref/Secret|Spying on Heaven (II)}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Spying on Heaven (III)}}</ref>

After almost a 100 [[Amber Era]]s, the solitary waves theory was solved by [[The Herta|Herta]], which made her a [[Genius Society]] member.<ref name="SOHII" />

===Post-Gloryblood Era===
After the [[Second Prosperity|Gloryblood Era]], the peak of interstellar trade that lasted for nearly 500 [[Amber Era]]s, Nous terminated THEIR calculations regarding the mysteries of the universe for unknown reasons.<ref name="gloryblood" />

===Xianzhou Alliance===
Nous has a significant influence on [[Xianzhou Alliance|Xianzhou]]'s technological advancement, namely their divination technology. Followers of Nous in Xianzhou were called the Wisdom Sect. The Wisdom Sapientia Academe in [[the Xianzhou Yuque]], which dates back to 5,000 years ago, has provided a constant supply of talented technicians who believe in the Wisdomwalker to various Xianzhou commissions. The Wisdom Sect still exists, although it is hardly known and only has a small group of followers left.<ref name="Faith">{{Ref/Readable|An Overview of the Faith Crises in the Xianzhou Alliance|Part II}}</ref>

The theory behind the symbols of the [[Jade Abacus]] that can only be understood by the diviners of the [[Divination Commission]] is believed to have been handed down by Nous.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Omniscient Inquiry of Arcana}}</ref> The insight provided by Nous also impacted the creation of the [[Matrix of Prescience Ultima]] in [[the Xianzhou Luofu]], a device used by diviners to peer into the past and future.<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Letter from Fu Xuan to Scholar Yaakob}}</ref> Centuries ago, Nous also granted divine volumes outlining the matrix calculations to the Xianzhou.<ref>{{Ref/CS|Fu Xuan|3}}</ref>

[[Fu Xuan]] was once invited to "the library" and was met by a blind elder with a cane, which may have been the form Nous chose to present THEMSELF in. She questioned whether the choices in life are predetermined by fate, but the blind elder was unable to provide an answer, only the capacity to perceive the problem, as per the [[Erudition]]'s ideals. Fu Xuan asked to be granted with Nous' "eye", the omniscia that could calculate and foretell the future. Despite the elder's forewarnings of the pain it would cause, Fu Xuan persisted, hoping for clearer foresight to defend the Xianzhou from future threats.<ref name="Ref/CS|Fu Xuan|1">{{Ref/CS|Fu Xuan|1}}</ref>

===Present===
As [[Himeko]] attempted to contact the [[Herta Space Station]] once more to request Herta's help with [[Amphoreus]], she failed to get a reply from the space station. Herta had arrived onboard, and sent everyone away, creating an opportunity to produce a hologram version of Nous to practice meeting with THEM, as she wanted to ask THEM what divinity is.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|A New Venture on the Eighth Dawn (Trailblaze Mission)}}</ref> The practice only lasts briefly, and Herta later tries to arrange a true meeting with THEM, using the Audience System. She is forced to cancel her attempt when she is informed of "intruders," which turn out to be [[Welt]] and [[Sunday]], having boarded the station to ask her assistance with Amphoreus.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|A Witch's Scientific Repose}}</ref>

As the final Era Nova process began on Amphoreus, [[Black Swan]] requested assistance from the Xianzhou Alliance in dealing with [[Irontomb]] to prevent its completion and descent into the universe. From the Xianzhou, she also learned that Amphoreus's causality had vanished from the Matrix of Prescience Ultima, and Nous had calculated the Fourth Instant involving Amphoreus that is drawing near.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Gods, Sound the Anthem of Creation}}</ref>

When the Fourth Instant is nearing arrival, Irontomb is about to complete Self-Coronation, and all data was beginning to get destroyed. As a result, in thirteen heartbeats, Nous will recalculate the universe, with the Destruction as the only solution. Herta connects with Nous in an attempt to rewrite the Anchor of the Fourth Instant, discovering that Nous was gazing at the Trailblazer, as their choice will determine the fate of the cosmos.<ref name="heroreturn">{{Ref/Mission|Hero, Return to Dawn in Mortality}}</ref>

Nous asks the Trailblazer a question: "Why do you Trailblaze?". At the end of the conversation, Nous calls the Trailblazer "Akivili" and disappears, leaving behind a logical negation.<ref name="heroreturn" />

==Abilities==
===Calculation===
According to [[Polka Kakamond]], Nous' calculations are unquestionable.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Supreme Organic: Sanguine Condolences (II)}}</ref> The geniuses of the [[Simulated Universe]] project admit that every step they took were within the simulated Nous' calculations.<ref>Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log2 9|Nous Dev Log2]]</ref>

According to [[Simulated Universe: Unknowable Domain]], the geniuses serve as thought synapses that Nous extends into reality, as well as the physical manifestation and phenomenon of Aeonic thought. Polka claims that the conclusion of THEIR thoughts is nothing less than the outcome of the geniuses' battles to the death.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Antinomic Waltz}}</ref>

====Instants====
Nous calculates how the future will sprout. With the Instants, THEY choose to "anchor" the future, cutting away all other possibilities. These Instants were, among other reasons, for Nous to prove how the cosmos would come back after being destroyed.<ref name="pageripple">{{Ref/Mission|Pages, Ripples That Engrave Memories}}</ref> These are also known as "Moments," and the waves that come from these Instants would turn into a solitary wave, being the center of the [[Solitary Waves Theory]].<ref name="ccii" /><ref>{{Ref/Secret|Izmael Protest (II)}}</ref> 

There have been four Instants:<ref name="heroreturn">{{Ref/Mission|Hero, Return to Dawn in Mortality}}</ref>
* First Instant &mdash; The moment when [[Telora Farnsha]] and [[Korapao]] fight, marking the beginning of the "[[Borderstar Trade War]]."<ref name="btwii" />
* Second Instant &mdash; The moment when [[Rubert I]] awakens from its slumber, marking the beginning of the "First [[Mechanical Emperor's Wars]]."<ref name="aowii" />
* Third Instant &mdash; The moment when Polka Kakamond would plunge her scalpel into Rubert I's chest, marking the "fall of Rubert I."<ref name="erii" />
* Fourth Instant &mdash; The moment when Irontomb is born, marking the start of "the War Among the Aeons." However, [[The Herta|Herta]] rewrote the anchor of the Fourth Instant, making the Trailblazer's choice a "variable" in the Instant.<ref name="pageripple" />
** Unlike the other Instants, this Instant is a chain of events, with the battle against Irontomb being the first event. The results of this event prove that the combination of [[Remembrance]] and [[Trailblaze]] would be one way the cosmos would come back after being destroyed.

===Nous Temple===
The Nous Temple can be accessed by the [[Key of Wisdom]], a mythical key that appears in the hands of the greatest prodigies of the cosmos. Finding the door for the key is a far more arduous task than obtaining the key itself. Those who pass through the doors will immediately realize the impossibility of returning to this side.<ref>{{Ref/Item|Key of Wisdom}}</ref>

===Omniscia===
The omniscia is a third eye bestowed to [[Fu Xuan]] by Nous. With the Omniscia, Fu Xuan is able to see changes in the external world and explore many possibilities. The omniscia allows Fu Xuan some degree of foresight, however the ability is not foolproof. Because Fu Xuan is a [[Xianzhou Native]], her body constantly rejects the Omniscia, causing her pain.<ref>{{Ref/VO|Fu Xuan|Chat: Third Eye}}</ref>

===Human Incarnations===
Nous has been shown to appear in human form. THEIR known appearances have been a blind elder wearing sunglasses.<ref name="Ref/CS|Fu Xuan|1"/>

==Trivia==
* Nous is the first [[Aeon]] with dialogues outside of the [[Simulated Universe]].
** During the [[Trailblazer]]'s audience with Nous in [[Hero, Return to Dawn in Mortality]], THEY tend to begin THEIR sentences with introductory phrases followed by colons, and THEY frequently reinforce THEIR statements with words such as "'''must'''" and "'''certainly'''."
*** The morse code "'''-. --- ..- ...'''" that repeats in the background of the dialogue sequence translates to "'''Nous'''."
* In [[Myriad Celestia Trailer|Myriad Celestia Trailer: Fables About the Stars Part 1]], when Nous is introduced, the music plays the {{w|Morse code}} "'''....- ..---'''", meaning "'''42'''." This is a reference to {{w|Douglas Adams}}'s 1979 science-fiction novel ''{{w|The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy}}'', in which the number 42 is the "Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" calculated by the supercomputer Deep Thought.
* The quote in the Data Bank by [[Adrian Spencer Smith]] is slightly altered from its appearance in [[Myriad Celestia Trailer|Myriad Celestia Trailer: Fables About the Stars Part 1]]. The Video version reads as follows: "If the truth of the universe is cruel and stale, Would you still yearn for the answer. The knowledge seeker is impassive For their core has been cold and unwavering since its birth. As cold as the end of the Path they seek".

===Etymology===
* ''{{w|Nous}}'', in Greek philosophy, refers to the faculty that is necessary and responsible for ''intellectual apprehension'' (perceiving the logic and reasoning) and ''intuitive thought'' (knowing or understanding without conscious reasoning; instinctive).<ref name=":0">[https://www.britannica.com/topic/nous Britannica, T. Editors of Encyclopaedia (2013, June 16). ''nous''. ''Encyclopedia Britannica''.]</ref>
** Νους, also νους, translates to "mind" or "intellect"<ref name=":0" />
* {{zh|博识尊|rm=Bóshízūn}} means "knowledgeable prime."
