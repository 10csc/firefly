'''Idrila''' is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Beauty]]. THEY disappeared sometime ago, and are considered deceased by the [[Mirror Holders]].

==Description==
{{Transclude|Beauty|Description}}

==Profile==
The pure goodness of the material world sensed and defined by intelligent lifeforms &mdash; this is how the so-called "beauty" came into existence.

According to an ancient myth, Idrila once claimed sole attribution for the beauty of all the starzones. THEY showcased the meaning and aesthetics within the cosmic landscape to heroes, villains, and mortals, driving them to accomplish astonishing but often devastating achievements for Idrila's own pleasure. This myth potentially unveils the true significance behind the path of Beauty: the integration of consciousness, insight, and values.<ref name="Glimpses" />

==History==
Idrila was born after having observed the ultimate meaning of the world's existence, that of "Beauty."<ref name="beauty special">HoYoLab: [https://www.hoyolab.com/article/23288935 Pom-Pom's Intel | The Beauty Special]</ref> THEY were said to ascend into Aeonhood in the "Agate World" [[Melustanin]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|The Sun Also Rises}}</ref> 

Idrila disappeared some time ago in the [[Consternation Starzone]], [[Lafcadio]].<ref>{{Ref/Curio|Road of Prayers}}</ref> The [[Knights of Beauty]] believes THEY'VE disappeared<ref name="beauty special" /> while the [[Mirror Holders]] believe THEM to be shattered.<ref name="MH Data Bank">{{Ref/Data Bank|Mirror Holders &mdash; Beauty}}</ref> The Mirror Holders claim of Idrila's fall seems to be the more agreed consensus, with [[Fu Xuan]]<ref name="Glimpses" /> and the [[Astral Express]]'s [[Data Bank]] believing Idrila to have fallen.<ref name="MH Data Bank" />

Long ago, Idrila blessed the planet [[Mendasia]], inhabited by followers of Beauty, with a shield called the Wall of Mirrors that concealed them from the stars and protected them from danger. After THEIR fall, the shield gradually crumbled until its full collapse approximately two [[Amber Era]]s ago, and the world was swallowed by an interstellar beast.<ref>NPC: [[Larose]]</ref>

The Mirror Holders believe Idrila could be revived through the collection of THEIR relics, and their claim seems to be supported through their efforts. They possess Mirrors of Transcendence which they claim are pieces of Idrila's body, and they continue to travel the universe collecting pieces of their Aeon.<ref name="MH Data Bank" /> Fu Xuan along with the [[Intelligentsia Guild]] believe the Mirror Holders' plan could work.<ref name="Glimpses" />
