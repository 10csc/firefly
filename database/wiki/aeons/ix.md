'''IX''' (pronounced /aɪ-ɛks/ 'eye-ex') is an [[Aeon]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' representing the [[Path]] of [[Nihility]].

==Description==
{{Transclude|Nihility|Description}}

==Profile==
IX is an apathetic Aeon, believing existence is absolutely meaningless, so doing anything is worthless. THEY don’t interact with other Aeons and THEIR existence is a mystery. Just being near IX’s form can make one feel numbed and dejected.<ref>Simulated Universe, Occurrence: [[Simulated Universe/Occurrence#Nihility: IX|The Nihility: IX]]</ref>

===Appearance===
IX resembles a large purple blob with two white eyes, with a large collection of asteroids and planets being pulled towards THEM. What appears to be THEIR body is a swirling nebula of magenta and blue stars enveloped by a large purple gas.

==Abilities==
===Horizon of Existence===
{{Main|Horizon of Existence}}
The Horizon of Existence is one of the thousands of manifestations belonging to IX, and it's also an exit out of the Nihility for the awake ones. It is depicted as a monochrome space with a liquid surface and a dark sun. As the watcher guarding the path to the abyss of the Nihility, [[Acheron]] is able to drag people into this place with her unleashed power or guide them back to reality.<ref name="8th">{{Ref/Mission|And on the Eighth Day}}</ref>

Known people who have been to the Horizon of Existence via Acheron include [[Aventurine]],<ref>{{Ref/Mission|A Walk Among the Tombstones}}</ref> the [[Trailblazer]],<ref name="8th" /> and [[Firefly]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Every Dawn of the World}}</ref>

===Shadows of IX===
{{Main|Shadow of IX}}
Shadows of IX or Shadows of Nihility are undefined creations of IX that exist in many forms. Its most prominent trait is the creation of [[Self-Annihilators]], beings who have entered the Shadow of IX and from that moment have lost the meaning of their existence.<ref name="Self-A's DB">{{Ref/Data Bank|Self-Annihilator &mdash; Nihility}}</ref> Shadows of IX can appear in numerous forms, such as the "Black Great Sun" which threatened to destroy [[Izumo]] and [[Takamagahara]]<ref name="Rondo Across">{{Ref/YouTube|Animated Short: Rondo Across Countless Kalpas|date=Apr 3 2024}}</ref> before [[Acheron]] destroyed both planets.<ref>{{Ref/CS|Acheron|2}}</ref> Another Shadow of IX appeared in a [[Memory Bubble]] sealed away by a faceless woman in a Memory Zone.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Gondola: Helping Gods!}}</ref> This was later broken open by a Self-Annihilator and eventually destroyed by a group of [[Arbitrators]].<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Lepismat System: Massacre Saga}}</ref>

The Self-Annihilators' connections to Shadows of IX goes deeper than just a form of transformation. As theorized by [[Dr. Primitive]], as Self-Annihilators aimlessly traverse the cosmos, they are also casting the shadow of the Aeon around the universe. As for the few who can single-handedly withstand the encroachment of Nihility on their existences, their journey of self-annihilation is drawn out to infinity, and the road they walk is like a shadow of IX cast in the world.<ref name="Self-A's DB" /> When the [[Doctors of Chaos]] created the [[Simulated Universe/Curio#Tonic of Efficacious Chaos|Tonic of Efficacious Chaos]], one of its side effects was causing Self-Annihilators to behold a Shadow of IX.<ref>{{Ref/Curio|Tonic of Efficacious Chaos}}</ref>

===Sin Thirsters===
{{Main|Sin Thirster}}
Deceased beings, usually the obsessions of the [[Pathstrider]]s, may emerge as Sin Thirsters from the depths of IX, seeing themselves as masters of their own destiny, unknowingly repeating the actions of their past lives. They emerge from the Nihility and head toward it, leading purposeless lives.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Bullet & Brain}}</ref>

==Trivia==
===Etymology===
* IX's name may derive from {{w|The Hermit (tarot card)|The Hermit (IX)}}, the ninth {{w|Major Arcana}} card in {{w|tarot}} decks.
* The pronunciation of IX may be a pun on ''exist'', as {{W|nihilism}} commonly refers to {{w|existential nihilism}} in popular use.
** In voice-overs, IX is pronounced ''yīkèsī'' in Chinese and ''ikkusu'' in Japanese.
