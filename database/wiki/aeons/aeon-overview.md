The '''Aeons''' are mysterious, godlike higher-dimensional beings in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]'' who preside over the universe.

==Profile==
The birth of an Aeon gives rise to a [[Path]], which the Aeon then has power over. Aeons also have the ability to bestow access to THEIR power, making a mortal an [[Emanator]] of THEIR Path. Aeons can only operate according to THEIR "Primum Mobile" in such that THEY are incapable of doing anything contrary to THEIR Path.<ref>[[Parlor Car]] Dialogue: [[Himeko/Dialogue|Himeko]]</ref> The gazes of the Aeons can be felt by those that have been gazed.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Everything that Rises Must Converge}}</ref> Most of the time, a gaze is simply a natural phenomenon, but some pathstriders regard it as a supreme honor.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Great Tomb, Hide the Secrets of Incarnations}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Relic|Grand Duke's Ceremonial Boots}}</ref>

===Mortality===
There are three known ways for an Aeon to die.<ref name="askew">{{Ref/Mission|Wood Sprouts Anew, Fate Tilts Askew}}</ref> 

# '''Through the collision of Paths stemming from overlapping concepts.''' &mdash; This is how [[Ena]] was assimilated by [[Xipe]], in a process [[Herta]] compares to a "survival of the fittest". However, there have examples of "similar" Paths co-existing for different lengths of time, such as the [[Preservation]] with the [[Remembrance]], and the [[Abundance]] with the [[Propagation]].<ref>Simulated Universe, The Destruction: Nanook: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log1 5|Dev Log1]]</ref> 
# '''[[War Among the Aeons|An Aeon killing another Aeon]].''' &mdash; While Kafka used Tayzzyronth as an example, it is later revealed that Tayzzyronth was merely sealed in amber and imprisoned, rendering this method dubious.<ref name="glimpses">{{Ref/Readable|Glimpses into the Beyond}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Swarm Disaster: Exchanging Secret Intel}}</ref>
# '''Forcing an Aeon to turn away from THEIR own Path.''' &mdash; Ashveil assumes this is [[Asat Pramad]]'s method of killing Aha.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Arcadia of Mystery and Terror}}</ref>

===Appearance===
The forms of the Aeons that is visible to the mortal eye are assumed to be THEIR projection from a higher-dimensional plane.<ref>Dialogue: [[Mare#Dialogue|Mare]]</ref> This is further supported in [[Dan Shu]]'s diary when she describes how [[Lan]] "descended onto the physical plane," which suggests that Lan had not been present in the mortal realm until THEY were summoned by distress signal sent by the previous [[Yuque]]'s Master Diviner.<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Dan Shu's Diary}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/CS|Fu Xuan|4}}</ref>

===Categories===
The [[Xianzhou Alliance|Xianzhou]]'s [[Divination Commission]] sorts Aeons into three categories:<ref name="glimpses" />
* '''"The Arbiters"''' &mdash; who determine mortal births and deaths, and are highly connected to the rise and fall of civilizations. [[Lan]] and [[Qlipoth]] are among these.<br/>
* '''"The Sacrosancts"''' &mdash; who are difficult to predict as good or evil, with THEIR ultimate destinations often unknowable. [[Nous]], [[Akivili]], [[Aha]], [[Idrila]], and [[Fuli]] are among these.<br/>
* '''"The Authors of Calamity"''' &mdash; who are the main culprit behind all disasters. [[Yaoshi]], [[Nanook]], and [[Tayzzyronth]] are among these.

==Trivia==
* In {{w|Gnosticism}}, Aeons are one of a class of powers or beings conceived as emanating from the Supreme Being and performing various functions in the operations of the universe.
* From [[Version 2.1]] onwards, the pronouns used to refer to Aeons in the English localization were changed from lowercase "they/them" to uppercase "THEY/THEM." This is likely an example of {{w|reverential capitalization}} being used to more accurately reflect the original Chinese word {{zh|祂|rm=tā}} being a gender-neutral pronoun for deities, rather than the standard {{zh|他}} or {{zh|她}} used for people.

===Etymology===
* The Chinese term for Aeons, {{zh|星神|rm=Xīngshén}}, means "God(s) of the Stars."
* Primum Mobile, the "law" that restricts the Aeons, comes from the {{w|Primum Mobile}}, meaning "first movable" in Latin.
