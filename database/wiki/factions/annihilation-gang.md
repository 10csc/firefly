The '''Annihilation Gang''' is a [[faction]] following the [[Aeon]] of [[Destruction]], [[Nanook]]. The Annihilation Gang has never earned their Aeon's gaze,<ref name="mundane">{{Ref/Mission|Mundane Troubles}}</ref> an objective they are still attempting to fulfill.<ref>{{Ref/Relic|Grand Duke's Ceremonial Boots}}</ref>

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Annihilation Gang &mdash; Destruction|Factions}}

==Overview==
The faction is comprised of numerous groups all dedicating themselves to the [[Path]] of Destruction. [[Genius Society]] #83 [[Herta]] describes the Annihilation Gang as full of "trash" that were too afraid to join the [[Antimatter Legion]].<ref name="SU">Simulated Universe, Aeons: [[Simulated Universe/Aeons#Dev Log2 6|IX Dev Log2]]</ref>

While they all belong in the same faction, there is a clear squabble among the groups, such as the [[Ever-Flame Mansion]] and [[Jepella Brotherhood]], who were enemies prior to the former's supposed dissolution.<ref name="mundane" />
