The '''Galaxy Rangers''' is a voluntarily formed group that travels around the cosmos to uphold justice for the locals out of the belief that benevolence and justice must be upheld by personal action.

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Galaxy Rangers &mdash; The Hunt|Factions}}

==Overview==
While members of the Galaxy Rangers hail from different worlds, and have all kinds of personalities and traits, each of them are highly skilled, despises evil, and follows [[Lan]] and the [[Path]] of [[The Hunt]].

They carry a balance scale with them at all times and believe justice in the universe needs to be achieved through individual deeds. They are always accompanied by the song and the sound of horns. They pursue justice for those they encounter along the way and shower them with wines and songs.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Punklorde Mentality}}</ref> 

According to [[Aventurine]], the Rangers "come and go like shadows," and there's little communication between them.<ref name="Aventurine">{{Ref/Mission|Whodunit}}</ref> [[Boothill]] doesn't think the Galaxy Rangers can even be considered an "organization" to begin with as they all follow their own path along The Hunt, with their own resolute sense of righteousness, and don't share so-called universal values.

The Galaxy Rangers don't need a form of shared faith. What brings them together is a shared bottom line: Never bully the weak, never kill the innocent. These oaths aren't beliefs, but rather the fundamental bottom line that one must never cross as a person. As Galaxy Rangers, they abide by a common principle and strictly adhere to the bottom line.<ref name="rob">{{Ref/Mission|The Great Train Robbery}}</ref> 

The Galaxy Rangers have relics in the form of a bullet that appear meaningless to anyone other than a Galaxy Ranger, that can only fulfill its purpose when returned to its rightful owner. This is because it's a burial artifact, worthy only of a hero who has served the Galaxy Rangers with honor. The relic is shot out like a flare and when it is used, it means the fall of a hero, and in the direction it's light falls, Galaxy Rangers will come from all corners of the Cosmos, driven by a shared purpose, without questioning the cause or counting the cost.<ref name="Eighth Day">{{Ref/Mission|And on the Eighth Day}}</ref>

==History==
==="Operation Zulo"===

The Galaxy Rangers rose to prominence following the successful assassination of [[Lord Ravager]] [[Zulo]]<ref name=":SoDF Rangers">Seat of Divine Foresight, Interactable: [[Seat of Divine Foresight#File Repository: Ally — Rangers|File Repository: Ally — Rangers]]</ref> in the operation later known as "Operation Zulo."<ref name="Loretta">Assignments: [[Assignments#Destruction of the Destroyer|Destruction of the Destroyer]]</ref> 

Prior to the operation, Galaxy Rangers had saved the life of [[Breukelen Tiernan]], a [[Nameless]] who fought for [[Penacony]]'s independence from the [[Interastral Peace Corporation]]. Alongside [[Loretta]], Tiernan remains one of the few known Galaxy Rangers to have directly battled Zulo,<ref name="Loretta" /> though he too vanished shortly afterward.<ref name="Eighth Day" />

Led by La Mancha, now known as Ashveil, the Rangers launched an assault on Zulo, who was using [[Kronstadt]] as a breeding ground for his forces.<ref name="letter">{{Ref/Readable|Letters to the Detective}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Stories That Must Be Told About the Boss of Galaxy Rangers}}</ref> To gain an advantage, the Rangers manipulated the remnants of [[the Swarm]], exploiting their lack of intelligence and herding them toward Zulo's fleet.<ref name=":SoDF Rangers" /><ref>Aurum Alley, Interactable: [[Aurum Alley#Mini Xiyan|Mini Xiyan]]</ref> Despite this tactic, the Galaxy Rangers were ultimately overwhelmed by Zulo's legion. As a final measure, Ashveil unleashed the "shadow" within his right arm, which consumed Zulo, the Swarm remnants, and many Galaxy Rangers alike. Although the operation succeeded in eliminating Zulo, it came at a devastating cost.<ref>{{Ref/CS|Ashveil|3}}</ref> In the aftermath, Ashveil incurred the ire of his fellow Rangers, most notably the other two Lead Hunters, "Heartless Bear" and "One-Eyed Owl," who accused him of dragging the Rangers into ruin for the sake of a personal vendetta. Following the incident, Ashveil quietly disappeared.<ref name="letter" />

Riding the success of "Operation Zulo", the Galaxy Rangers would next defeat the Thirteenth Legion of Gaius the Usurper, the successor of [[Genius Society]] Member #27, [[Rubert I|Emperor Rubert]]'s will. However, their means of accomplishing this are unknown.<ref name=":SoDF Rangers" />

===Dr. Primitive===
Many years later, the Galaxy Rangers would thwart the 64th member of the Genius Society, [[Dr. Primitive]], and protect the Thousand-Tree Greenwood on [[Vonwacq]]. For this, some claim that many Galaxy Rangers would suffer from Degeneration Flow and lose their combat capabilities resulting in the Galaxy Rangers having to cut back on their efforts.<ref name=":SoDF Rangers" /> Dr. Primitive however took an unknown form of revenge on the Galaxy Rangers, further pushing the Galaxy Rangers into obscurity.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|When the Stars of Ingenuity Shine}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Galaxy Rangers &mdash; The Hunt}}</ref>

Presently, the Galaxy Rangers and Dr. Primitive are closely intertwined, and even after his rumored death, there are still Rangers who hunt for him.<ref>{{Ref/Relic|Genius's Gravity Walker}}</ref> Once, an individual believed to be a Galaxy Ranger sold [[Simulated Universe/Curio#The Doctor's Robe|Dr. Primitive's robes]] on [[Device IX|Device IX's]] dark web but refused to answer any questions.<ref>{{Ref/Curio|The Doctor's Robe}}</ref>

At some point in the past, the Galaxy Rangers were alerted to a facility run by Laboratory 17 of the [[AtaviSearch Forest]], after [["Master Kucha"|one of its researchers]] became disillusioned after seeing how the others intended to get rid of a promising subject, AK-A-3. After he killed his [["Umbra Headmistress"|colleague]] (who had tried to get rid of her numerous times), the Galaxy Rangers discovered AK-A-3 and her unnatural powers, given to her through experimentation. AK-A-3 gave herself the name [[Rappa]] and joined the Galaxy Rangers in order to hunt down the Evil Ninja Osaru.<ref name="MonkeyTail">{{Ref/Mission|The Men Who Tread on the Monkey's Tail}}</ref>

==Present==
While the Galaxy Rangers have fallen into a state of scarcity,<ref name="Aventurine" /> they are still an active group in the universe and have appeared numerous times in recent events.

[[Colleen]] recounts a story about a pirate she used to work with called Wyland, who replaced most of his body with prosthetics. After a masked visitor informed him that void song whale brain extract could relieve Wyland of his headaches due to old age, Wyland begun to massacre void song whales. He hooked his nervous system up to his ship for maximum efficiency, and at this point the [[IPC]] reached out to the Galaxy Rangers to put a stop to him. However, when all their forces arrived Wyland was already dead.<ref>NPC dialogue: [[Colleen]]</ref>

Another time, an anonymous Ranger claimed that [[The Cremators]] were behind the Holstein Aphasia in 2157 [[AE]], in which the residents of Holstein had all their memories relating to "language" stolen. Recently, the Cremators were confirmed to be behind it, and the Galaxy Rangers have announced that the rangers are working on retrieving the stolen memories.<ref>Parlor Car, Interactable: [[Parlor Car#IPC Broadcast #2|IPC Broadcast 2]]</ref>

At some point, a Galaxy Ranger chased a group of interstellar thieves into [[Talia]]. These thieves would later go on to create [[Nailscrap Town]].<ref>{{Ref/Relic|Talia's Nailscrap Town}}</ref> At some point in time, the [[Xianzhou Yuque]] started looking for the leader of the Galaxy Rangers, [[La Mancha]], hoping to rebuild a connection with the hero.<ref name=":SoDF Rangers"/>

===Penacony===
The [[Self-Annihilator]] [[Acheron]] arrived on Penacony posing as a Galaxy Ranger, which caused [[Boothill]], a real Galaxy Ranger, to seek her out and try to kill her.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|When the Sacred Ginmill Closes}}</ref> Upon meeting Acheron himself, she explains to him that given the group's elusive nature and limited contact with each other, she had to devise a plan to "reach out" to him. She had done so in order to fulfill a long-standing promise to return [[Tiernan]]'s relics to their rightful owner.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Bullet & Brain}}</ref> Boothill would use Tiernan's relics to summon Galaxy Rangers from all over the cosmos to the Asdana system to weaken [[Sunday]]'s dream, which alerted the cosmos at large that the Galaxy Rangers had resurfaced from obscurity. Boothill would later confront [[Aventurine]] in order to learn the whereabouts of [[Oswaldo Schneider]] to continue his original mission of getting vengeance on the man who destroyed his planet and people.<ref name="Eighth Day" /><ref>{{Ref/CS|Boothill|4}}</ref>

Boothill would later attend the [[Luminary Wardance]] on the Luofu seeing that the IPC had sponsored it. Though the IPC ordered his arrest, the Luofu declined to do so. Boothill briefly fought [[Luka]] and defeated him in a quick duel, eventually facing [[Yanqing]] in the semi-finals. He forfeited quickly into the match, knowing that the IPC were after him. After ordering a drink for Luka as an apology, he left the Luofu.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Even When Cheers Fade: Chapter 5}}</ref> Rappa also accompanied Boothill during this time, observing his duel against Luka.<ref>{{Ref/VO|Rappa|About the Red Oni With the Ninja Prosthetic Arm}}</ref>

Rappa would later have Boothill accompany her to Penacony after tracking Dr. Primitive to the planet, discovering a plot to turn the planet's populace into monkeys. With the assistance of the Memokeeper [[Mr. Reca]], [[Robin]] and the Astral Express, they were able to foil [["Profnana"]]'s plan, though he escaped. After the incident was over, Mr. Reca approached Rappa and gave her remuneration for helping his "film" by giving her information on where Dr. Primitive had gone.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Battles Without Ninja and Humanity}}</ref>

===Amphoreus===
As [[Irontomb]]'s birth became inevitable, the Galaxy Rangers responded to the [[Astral Express]]'s call, forming a [[Cosmic Alliance]] with factions such as the [[Strategic Investment Department]] of [[Interastral Peace Corporation]] and the [[Xianzhou Alliance]] to oppose Irontomb. The Galaxy Rangers were involved in coordinating communication with worlds beyond the [[Pan-Cosmic Trade System]], rescuing people from nearby star systems,<ref>Pre-War Newsflash: [[Pre-War Newsflash|Part One | Formation of the Cosmic Alliance]]</ref> and engaging monsters on all fronts.<ref>Pre-War Newsflash: [[Pre-War Newsflash|Part Four | Real-time Battle Communication]]</ref>

===Planarcadia===
Following the defeat of Irontomb, the Galaxy Rangers were one of the factions invited to join the [[Cosmic Alliance]]. Around this time, the Galaxy Rangers had also gathered to track down their leader, La Mancha,<ref>{{Ref/YouTube|myriad_trailer = Anti-Destruction Alliance|date = 2026-03-26}}</ref> who was currently laying low under the identity of Ashveil.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Echoes from a Sombre Empire}}</ref> During the [[Phantasmoon Games]], [[Pearl]]'s planning caused Ashveil to reveal himself and his powers to the Astral Express Crew.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Real Holy Laughter!}}</ref>
