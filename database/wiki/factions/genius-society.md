The '''Genius Society''' is an intergalactic organization, whose 84 members are personally selected by [[Nous]] the [[Erudition]] from across the cosmos, and is a major faction in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]''.

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Genius Society &mdash; Erudition|Factions}}

==Overview==
The Genius Society is a gathering of geniuses across the cosmos who had drawn the attention of Nous the Erudition, founded by first member [[Zandar One Kuwabara]]. Many often misinterpret the invitation as only an approval. The few who are able to understand the Aeon's intentions are given a key to the halls of the [[Nous Temple]]. Those who pass through the gates seek guidance from Nous and leave behind their legacy to never return.<ref>{{Ref/Item|Key of Wisdom}}</ref>

Besides their intellect, members of the society are also known for how much they differ from each other, whether it be in terms of background, ideals, or goals. Most geniuses in the society are lone wolves, asocial, and people with social disorders whom refuse to communicate with each other, and only a few &mdash; such as [[Aiden]], [[Elias Salas]], and [[Herta]]<ref>Parlor Car: [[Parlor Car#IPC Broadcast #1|IPC Broadcast #1]]</ref> &mdash; are willing to share their findings and use their talent to contribute to the advancement of the cosmos, preferring to indulge in their research for enjoyment.<ref name="Herta M">{{Ref/Readable|Herta's Manuscripts|Preface}}</ref> Some are even lunatics, like [[Rubert I]], who dedicated itself to eradicate all organic life forms in the universe,<ref name="DB Rubert">{{Ref/Data Bank|Rubert the Emperor (Character)|Terms}}</ref> or [[Polka Kakamond]], known for killing fellow members of the society who approach the borders of the [[Circle of Knowledge]].<ref name="Polka Portr">Herta's Office, Interactable: [[Herta's Office#Polka's Portrait|Polka's Portrait]]</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Antinomic Waltz}}</ref> Despite the lack of structure within the organization, it "has its own rules", with one of those being that the Genius Society won't interfere with each other's research topics.<ref name="Herta M" /><ref name="ardentresearch">{{Ref/Mission|The Witch's Ardent Research}}</ref>

Due to the tampering of the [[History Fictionologists]] and the preference for solitude among most of its members,<ref name="Sensing Visor">{{Ref/Relic|Genius's Ultraremote Sensing Visor}}</ref> the history of the organization is mostly shrouded in mystery.
