==Structure==
The Family's exact structure and governance is unknown, but seems to vary based on world. Penacony's branch was divided into five major families, a structure that preceded the Family's presence on the planet.{{cite}} Given this, it is possible that the Family adapts to its member world's local societies, while adding its own systems and influence, as seen with the [[Dreammaster]], who was a representative of the [[Montour System]]'s Family branch sent to oversee Penacony's integration. [[Sunday]] refers to Penacony as "just a small territory,"<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Bow Out At Moment of Début}}</ref> so it is possible that planets deeper in Family territory may hold greater influence over the faction overall.

===Scale Degree System===
The Family treats everyone equally without division of class, with no established levels or power structure. Each note holds equal significance in the music Xipe has composed. However, for collective progress, someone must assume a leadership role. As a result, Family members have returned to a system of differentiation based on scales.<ref name="strings">{{Ref/Data Bank|Harmonic Strings (Phenomenon)}}</ref>

==History==
===Past===
The Family was formed some time after Xipe the Harmony's ascension during the [[Swarm Disaster]], and has grown over the years since to encompass countless worlds. The [[Beyond the Sky Choir]], a group following [[Ena]] the [[Order]], are implied to have integrated into the Family.{{cite}}

At some point, an unnamed man attended a grand theater hosted by the Family to investigate the happenings within the faction. The man unintentionally revealed his intentions while conversing with [[Renoir]], questioning the faction's existence. Without a direct response, Renoir called for a waiter and presented him with a peculiar box. Under the influence of Renoir's distorted voice, the man unconsciously reached his hand into the box, acquiring a [[Simulated Universe/Curio#Family Ties|Family Ties]]. The entire audience coldly glared him down while wearing identical, eerie smiles, and with the same voice, began to tempt him to join them. Frightened by this, he forced the same smile in a bid for survival and escaped the theater.<ref name="bubble">Memory Bubble: [[Memory Bubble: Family|Family]]</ref><ref>{{Ref/Curio|Family Ties}}</ref>
