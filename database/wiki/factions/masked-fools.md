The '''Masked Fools''' are an intergalactic organization devoted to [[Aha]], the [[Aeon]] of [[Elation]]. They operate out of [[The Tavern]].

==Description ==
{{Data Bank|Masked Fools &mdash; Elation|Factions}}

==Overview==
The Masked Fools possess the most radical philosophy out of all the followers of Elation: they believe that the ultimate truth of the world is nothing but a joke, and the profound meaning of everything lies in simple laughter.<ref name="hoyolab">{{Ref/HoYoLAB|Astral Express Magazine - Penacony Info Bulletin "Masked Fools"}}</ref> While they enjoy pursuing and obtaining joy, each member appears to have their own limits in doing so.<ref>{{Ref/VO|Sparkle|About Sampo}}</ref> They leave a trail of trouble and chaos wherever they go, and their goal is to bring about change in the stagnant pools of life.<ref name="hoyolab" />

Each member possesses a unique "[[Mourning Actor Masks|mask]]" belonging only to them, a "mask" bestowed by Aha. These masks allow them to obtain power from their [[Path]]. Caution is advised when interacting with individuals wearing masks, as their true intentions can be challenging to discern from their words and actions.<ref name="guide">[https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/guidetopenacony The Ultimate Trailblazer's Guide to Penacony]</ref>

==History==
[[Hosea Lazaro]] mentions having spent a great deal of time in bars run by the Fools.<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Letter From the Comet Hunter|Letter From the Comet Hunter (III)}}</ref> The [[Strange Plant]] claims that a Masked Fool has infiltrated [[Herta Space Station]], and that they're planning something huge.<ref>Interactable: [[Strange Plant]]</ref>

When the [[Astral Express Crew]] landed on [[Jarilo-VI]], [[Sampo]] was the first person to greet them. He later introduced the crew to [[Wildfire]] and guided them between the [[Overworld]] and [[Underworld]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Cleanse in the Darkness Outside}}</ref>

On [[the Xianzhou Luofu]], the [[Trailblazer]] encountered a Masked Fool named [[Zhongshan]] who sabotaged and stirred up chaos among the members of [[the Unshackled]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Bestial Ferocity}}</ref><ref name="letter">{{Ref/Readable|Zhongshan's Letter}}</ref>

The Masked Fool [[Sparkle]], among several others, was invited to the [[Charmony Festival]] on [[Penacony]] in 2158 AE. She assisted [[Aventurine]] and the Astral Express Crew in uncovering the conspiracy within [[The Family]]. It is later revealed that she was hired by [[Stellaron Hunter]] [[Silver Wolf]] to ensure that [[Firefly]]'s prophesied three deaths would happen in the safest way possible.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* The Epsilon XII superstar Ast Rickley is a reference to the pop singer {{w|Rick Astley}}, and the Song "{{w|Never Gonna Give You Up}}," which is associated with the "rickroll" meme.
* Prior to joining the [[IPC]], [[Aventurine]] was once invited to join the Masked Fools, but declined their offer.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Gentleness, the Name of Nocturne}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/VO|Sparkle|About Aventurine}}</ref>
* After orchestrating or involving themselves in a major plot, at least two Masked Fools have recited variations of the same line:
** '''Sampo:''' ''"To you, my dear audience, I dedicate my performance... I wonder, did I bring a little more joy into your lives? ...You don't have to answer that &mdash; but if the answer is no, then you'll break my heart."''<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Silent Galaxy}}</ref>
** '''Sparkle:''' ''"To you, my dear audience, I dedicate this performance... I wonder, did it bring a little more elation into your lives? If it didn't... don't sleep too soundly tonight."''<ref name="short">{{Ref/YouTube|Sweet Dreams Curtain Call - "The Great Tatalov Salutes You"|date=Jun 20 2024}}</ref>
* In the main story of ''{{w|Honkai Impact 3rd}}'' Version 7.8, Sparkle has gifted [[w:c:honkaiimpact3:Vita|Vita]] a mask and invited her to join the Masked Fools.
