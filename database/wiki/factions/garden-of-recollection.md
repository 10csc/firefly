The '''Garden of Recollection''', often shortened to just the '''Garden''', is a [[faction]] which worships the [[Aeon]] of [[Remembrance]], [[Fuli]]. The faction's '''Memokeepers''' take on the form of [[Memetic Entities]] and travel to different worlds to gather memories.<ref name="fleeting-lights">{{Ref/Mission|Fleeting Lights}}</ref>

==Description==
{{Data Bank|Garden of Recollection &mdash; Remembrance|Factions}}

==Overview==
As messengers for the Remembrance, Memokeepers surrender their worldly bodies and traverse the universe in the form of memories. The form their bodies take is known as a dharmakāya, a shell containing their consciousness.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Memokeeper, Backtrack the Destiny's Current}}</ref> These forms are far more resilient than other memetic entities, as it protects them from the forgetful nature of Remembrance and the corrosion of [[Enigmata]].<ref>NPC Dialogue: [[Graysen]]</ref> Their goal is to search the stars on Fuli's behalf to find the most precious memories, and to make sure the universe is remembered in the face of the [[Destruction]].<ref name="fleeting-lights" />

Memokeepers travel to worlds alone. Using their powers to prevent the inhabitants from knowing of their presence, they only reveal themselves if they come across someone who has the potential of being a Memokeeper, after which they will reveal themselves and offer the chance for recruitment.<ref name="swan2">{{Ref/CS|Black Swan|2}}</ref> Due to their elusive nature, Memokeepers of the Garden of Recollection have gained a fearsome reputation.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Should One Awaken at Midnight}}</ref>

Memokeepers follow an extremely strict set of rules in order to ensure that their powers are not misused, and are specifically trained to resist looking into, stealing, controlling, or restoring memories. Violating this rule results in their immediate termination, though a few members are given an exception to this rule, known as "Memosnatchers."<ref name="memokeeper">NPC Dialogue: [[Messenger]]</ref>

===Technology===
[[Memory Bubble]]s are a technology developed by the Garden of Recollection to preserve memories. The Garden created a Sentiention membrane to keep the memories wrapped up in it like a bubble, making it easy to carry memories around.<ref name="herta">NPC Dialogue: [[Memory Bubble#Dialogue|Herta]]</ref>

Similarly, [[Light Cones]] are technology that preserve memories created by the Garden, but on a more advanced scale. Because they also preserve experiences and abilities, this technology is under level-2 restriction by the [[Interastral Peace Corporation]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|A Moment of Peace}}</ref> The memories contained in Light Cones become clearer when infused with substances such as [[Sparse Aether]], [[Condensed Aether]], and [[Refined Aether]].<ref>{{Ref/Item|Sparse Aether}}</ref>

==Subgroups==
===The Cremators===
{{Main|The Cremators}}
While many of the faction hold onto the core beliefs of Remembrance, there exists an off-shot of Memokeepers known as "The Cremators" who don't believe in every memory being valuable. The Cremators will destroy memories they find unimportant in order to "clear out the garbage."<ref name="cremators">{{Ref/Data Bank|The Cremators — Remembrance}}</ref>

===Memosnatchers===
The Memosnatchers, according to [[The Herta|Herta]], are the radicals of the Garden of Recollection.<ref name="herta" /> They have the ability to avoid some of the rules of the Garden of Recollection and aren't completely aligned with other Memokeepers.<ref name="memokeeper" />

==History==
It is widely agreed that the Garden of Recollection was founded by one or several [[Pure Children of Anāsrava]].<ref name="child" />

Memokeepers were likely around sometime before the [[Swarm Disaster]]. Some of them managed to seal a [[Shadow of IX]] inside a Memory Bubble<ref name="gondola">{{Ref/Secret|Gondola: Helping Gods!|5}}</ref> and took it aboard a [[Mourning Actor]] gondola.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Gondola: Helping Gods!|1}}</ref> It remained there until the Swarm Disaster, where the gondola would find itself entering the Elydichna Starzone,<ref name="gondola" /> and the [[Self-Annihilator]] who caused [[Tayzzyronth]]'s ascension would board the gondola. The Self-Annihilator identified the Memory Bubble as his "lost self," and after it was broken and the Shadow of IX released, he embraced the Shadow, killing him. After his death, the Shadow of IX was resealed by the [[Arbitrators]].<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Lepismat System: Massacre Saga|6}}</ref>

After the Swarm Disaster, [[Elias Salas]] invented the [[Synesthesia Beacon]], which he later sold the rights for to the [[Intelligentsia Guild]].<ref>[[Herta's Office]], Interactable: [[Herta's Office#Elias Salas' Portrait|Elias Salas' Portrait]]</ref> During that time, the Interastral Peace Corporation terminated its existing contract with the Garden to make way for the new technology.<ref>{{Ref/Secret|Judged as Organic: Associates (I)}}</ref>

When [[Boothill]] received a new body to begin his hunt against [[Oswaldo Schneider]], who ordered a genocide on his people, he broke into a local Garden of Recollection branch to find memories related to his homeworld. He unintentionally destroyed multiple Memory Bubbles in the process, and the Memokeepers scrambled to recover as much rare and precious memories as possible before kicking him out. In the end, Boothill still managed to learn about Oswaldo Schneider,<ref>{{Ref/CS|Boothill|4}}</ref> and the Garden of Recollection still hold animosity towards him, even believing Boothill to be responsible for the [[Holstein]] Aphasia.<ref name="boothill" />

After learning of the [[Trailblazer]]'s existence, the Garden of Recollection took an interest in them due to their ability to hold a [[Stellaron]], and were curious to see how they would influence the universe.<ref name="memokeeper" />

At some point in time, the Garden of Recollection discovered [[Amphoreus]] through the reflections of their mirror, adding it to their private collection. [[Black Swan]], who figured out the Garden's intentions of learning the secrets of Amphoreus to remodel other worlds into a similar form, decided to approach the [[Astral Express Crew]] to trailblaze toward the planet, masking her true goal as simply being her desire to collect the memories of Amphoreus.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Silver Chariot, Away to that Blackened Land}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Scholar, Let Us Meet Again Before the Gates of Truth}}</ref>

A Memosnatcher later trailed behind Herta to steal [[Nous]]' memories by tampering with the Audience System to bait Herta into leading the Memosnatcher to THEM. However, Herta noticed, and she imprisoned the Memosnatcher in one of her Mirrors to suffer for a hundredth of Herta's lifetime. After her meeting with [[Welt]] and [[Sunday]], Herta probed into the mind of the imprisoned Memosnatcher and learned that many other Memokeepers of the Garden know of Amphoreus.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|A Witch's Scientific Repose}}</ref><ref>{{Ref/Mission|Witch's Mirrored Reversal}}</ref>

Due to the importance of Amphoreus, some members of the Garden tried to influence the Nameless in order to access it. This resulted in [[March 7th]] falling ill when the Astral Express arrived outside the system, which according to [[Cyrene]], was caused by the members manipulating and warping her memories to use against her. When March found herself in the [[Path Space]], the Garden attempted to convince March to enter Amphoreus by taking on the forms of various friends. Upon reaching the end, Cyrene warned her of their manipulation, telling her that if she stepped into Amphoreus, she could potentially lose her own fate. The manifestations claimed that if she stepped into Amphoreus, she could save the Garden. March was undeterred, knowing that her friends were in there and wanted to help them.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Hero, Honor That Crimson Call}}</ref>

When the Trailblazer was sealed by [[Lygus]], they were briefly reminded by Herta to figure out which of the four Aeons they had interacted with could help them escape confinement. The Trailblazer ultimately recalled the Remembrance, and several members of the Garden met with them disguised as March, only for the [[Evernight|"real" March]] to appear and force them away.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Wanderer, Decipher the Waxen Imprints}}</ref> 

It is revealed that several of the Garden's members sent to Amphoreus were under orders from a Pure Child of Anāsrava. Their real role was to act as a sacrifice so the Pure Child could locate her sisters, kill, and then "reclaim" them.<ref name="backtrack" />

Various memetic entities from the Garden of Recollection would also arrive on Amphoreus, only to be corrupted by Destruction's golden ichor, creating the Ichor Memosprites.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Blazing Sun, Illuminate the Path for the Lost}}</ref> Following the events in Amphoreus, the Garden of Recollection began issuing summons to its members, forcing Black Swan to depart from the Astral Express and cover up her tracks, as she had defied the organization.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Phantasmoon, the Merciful Queen}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* In the main story of Version 7.8 of ''{{w|Honkai Impact 3rd}}'' "The First and Last War," [[w:c:honkaiimpact3:Kiana Kaslana|Kiana Kaslana]] refused to trade her memory with a Memokeeper visiting her world, and the Memokeeper claimed that Kiana's power matches that of an [[Emanator]] despite no influence of [[Aeon]]s in her world.
* In {{Mission|Fleeting Lights}}, Welt erroneously states that "the Emanators of the Remembrance don't have a physical body and only those they allow can see them" when referring to the Messenger, implying that the Memokeepers of the Garden of Recollection are Emanators. It's ambiguous whether this is a retcon or Welt simply being incorrect with his sources, but story content later on disproves this statement, as Black Swan makes no mention of being an Emanator in relevant conversations with both [[Acheron]] and [[Himeko]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|And on the Eighth Day|quote='''Acheron:''' The [[Dominicus|Harmonious Choir]] possesses the power of an Emanator. To overcome it, you'll need the same level of power. The final step in destroying the sweet dream... will be my responsibility.<br />'''Black Swan:''' That's a relief to hear.}}</ref><ref name="scholar">{{Ref/Mission|Scholar, Let Us Meet Again Before the Gates of Truth|quote='''Black Swan:''' If those pathstriders of Remembrance were to uncover the secrets of Amphoreus first...<br />[...]<br />'''Himeko:''' Remodeling a world is no easy feat. These extremists likely aren't just simple pathstriders. Do they wield the powers of Emanators?<br />'''Black Swan:''' You can be even bolder with your guesses. The people that I'm talking about... They aren't just individuals. They are an organization that has already attached itself to the pinky finger of Fuli.}}</ref>
** The same can be said for Mr. Reca, as he temporarily worked with [["Profnana"]] and made no mention about being an Emanator, though he reveals himself to be a Memokeeper.
** In the same vein, Himeko theorizes that an extremist faction within the Garden of Recollection are Emanators, and Black Swan does not deny her conjecture.<ref name="scholar" />
