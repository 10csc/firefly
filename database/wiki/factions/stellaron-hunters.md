The '''Stellaron Hunters''' are a [[faction]] in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]''. Founded and led by [[Elio]], they walk the [[Path]] of [[Finality]] in an attempt to prevent the [[Theory of Four Apocalypses]].<ref name="SP" /><ref name="NW, StSP" /><ref name="T,FTBtI">{{Ref/Mission|Traveler, Find Truth Beyond the Illusion}}</ref> While they have collected and used numerous [[Stellaron]]s in the past, this is merely a front for their true intentions.<ref name="CItD">{{Ref/Mission|Chaos In the Deep}}</ref>

{{Data Bank|Stellaron Hunter &mdash; ???|Factions}}

==Overview==
The Stellaron Hunters are a band of powerful individuals united under [[Elio]], with the promise that each of them will fulfill their desires by following his script.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Letter from a Strange Woman}}</ref> They are said to work against the [[Interastral Peace Corporation]].<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Herta's Manuscripts}}</ref> Due to Elio's ability to see possibilties, the group is often privy to information that would normally be impossible to obtain, such as the specific times when events will occur,<ref name="CItD" /> or seemingly impossible butterfly effects.<ref>{{Ref/CS|Kafka|4}}</ref> With this information, the group plans to set up the exact series of events required to prevent the [[Theory of Four Apocalypses]].<ref name="T,FTBtI" /> This process heavily hinges on the [[Trailblazer]], as Elio has forseen that they will be the one to cause the [[Finality]], and ultimately be the one who will save the universe.<ref name="TWSB">{{Ref/Mission|That Which Shall Be}}</ref> Elio has been slowly preparing the Trailblazer to face [[Nanook]],<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Wood Sprouts Anew, Fate Tilts Askew}}</ref> and has chosen them, along with [[Firefly]] to be the last remaining survivors of [[Nihility]].<ref name"TDSiT">{{Ref/Mission|The Dream Split in Two}}</ref>{{Verify}}

The group is often seen with a black cat, whom the Trailblaze assumed to be Elio, but is later explained to be a fragment of [[Terminus]]. According to [[Constance]], Elio has a "special existence," and only the Stellaron Hunters are aware of his true identity.<ref name="TWSB" />

===Stellarons===
Despite the Stellarons simply being a front for the groups real intentions,<ref name="CItD" /> in the past, their efforts would often revolve around specific Stellarons. This changed upon the arrival of the Trailblazer.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Small Town Grotesque}}</ref><ref group="Note">While Firefly says the Stellaron Hunters used to revolve around the Stellarons before the Trailblazer arrived, as stated in {{Mission|The Dream Split in Two|showChapter=0}}, the Trailblazer was already a member before Firefly, Blade, and Silver Wolf joined. It is unclear if Firefly simply meant the Trailblazer's new identity following the loss of memories and placement on [[Herta Space Station]], or if she's alluding to the time before she was a member.</ref>
