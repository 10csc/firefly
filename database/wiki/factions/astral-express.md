The '''Astral Express''', also referred to as the '''Chromatic Echelon''' by the [[Xianzhou Alliance]],<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Glimpses into the Beyond}}</ref> is a large, spacefaring train that serves as one of the main settings of ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]''. It is both the means of travel and primary residence of the [[Astral Express crew]].

The Astral Express is not treated as its own world in [[Navigation]], but is instead accessible from every world's area list. Currently, three cars can be accessed in-game: the [[Parlor Car]], [[Party Car]], and the [[Passenger Cabin]]. A buffet car has been mentioned, but has not been seen.<ref>{{Ref/Data Bank|Astral Express &mdash; Trailblaze}}</ref>

==History==
The Astral Express was created by [[Akivili]], the [[Aeon]] of [[Trailblaze]], and used to transport THEM and [[the Nameless]] to multiple worlds. After Akivili's disappearance, it fell into disrepair and eventually crash-landed onto an unnamed planet. [[Himeko]] discovered the wreckage and repaired the Express, becoming its navigator and forming a new Astral Express crew. Their current goal is to retrace Akivili's steps, repairing the [[star rail]] THEY laid down thousands of years ago and sealing the [[Stellaron]]s threatening the galaxy. Legends claim that Akivili's heart powers the core of the Express, although Himeko has found no evidence to support this claim.<ref name="himeko">NPC Dialogue: [[Himeko/Dialogue|Himeko]]</ref>

Shortly after the fall of Akivili, a loyal group of Nameless took THEIR trailblazing legacy upon themselves. However, the star rail became obstructed as Stellarons began spreading across the galaxy, preventing the Express from running. It eventually fell into disrepair and was abandoned.

Some time later, the Express crash-landed onto [[Planarcadia]]<ref>{{Ref/YouTube|special_program=4.0|date=Feb 6 2026}}</ref> during [[Asat Pramad]]'s invasion, standing on the planet like a tower, and the Nameless on the Express emerged and began assisting the planet's natives in fending off the invasion. [[Graphia]], Asat Pramad's adoptive daughter, was inspired to defect from the [[Antimatter Legion]] and help the people of Ahatopia instead, which eventually led her to repel the invasion.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Best Farewell to the World}}</ref> Many years later, Himeko, Graphia's descendant, discovered the train and was inspired to repair it and take to the stars.<ref group="Note">Himeko directly contradicts her own statements when the Express first landed: her regular dialogue states that it crashed long before she was born, but after defeating [[Svarog]] in {{Mission|To Rot or to Burn|showChapter=0}}, she states that it crashed on her birthday when she was hiking outdoors. As of {{Mission|The Lethe Below the Living|showChapter=0}}, the former is discovered to be true.</ref> She successfully repaired the train after many years, and began retracing Akivili's steps through the universe, eventually recruiting [[Welt]], [[Void Archives]] (now departed), [[Dan Heng]], [[March 7th]], and the [[Trailblazer]] to the crew. The Express is currently traveling on a route that Akivili once embarked on, with its final destination being THEIR home world, [[Pegana]].<ref name="himeko" /><ref>{{Ref/Mission|An Invitation Without Proffer}}</ref>

As the Astral Express crew finished their adventure on [[Penacony]], Welt explained that the Express gets fuel by converting "Trailblaze" into the energy needed for it to run. As long as trailblazing expeditions continued without interruption, the Express would receive a constant flow of energy akin to a perpetual motion machine. However, due to their prior expeditions taking longer than anticipated, they only had enough fuel for two more warp jumps. To solve this problem, the [[Memokeeper]] [[Black Swan]] suggested they travel to a world that even Akivili had never reached &mdash; the Eternal Land, [[Amphoreus]]. If they could lay down a new stretch of the star rail to Amphoreus, the Express would never have to worry about fuel again.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>
