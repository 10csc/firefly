The '''Knights of Beauty''' are a [[Faction]] that follow the [[Aeon]] [[Idrila]], many believing THEM to be missing as opposed to deceased.

==Description==
{{Data Bank|The Knights of Beauty &mdash; Beauty|Factions}}

==Overview==
Among Idrila's followers, the Knights of Beauty are the most infatuated with classical beauty. They chant beautiful hymns on their mounts and endlessly roam the universe, extolling Idrila's name.<ref name="Hoyolab">Honkai: Star Rail (November 28, 2023). [https://www.hoyolab.com/article/23288935 Pom-Pom's Intel | The Beauty Special]. HoYoLAB. Retrieved April 7, 2024.</ref> While they tend to operate in solitude, they are known to also travel in groups.<ref name="sim">Simulated Universe Occurrence: [[Knights of Beauty to the Rescue]]</ref>

Due to the fractured nature of the faction, many of the Knights have taken up different stances on [[Beauty]] and Idrila,<ref name="Argenti 4">{{Ref/CS|Argenti|4}}</ref> however they all share the same Code of Chivalry.<ref>{{Ref/VO|Argenti|Chat: The Knights of Beauty}}</ref> There exists different groups of Knights, however, the only one currently known is Honorclad, of which [[Argenti]] is a part of. According to Argenti, the Honorclad can be distinguished by their engraved armor.<ref>{{Ref/VO|Argenti|About Self: Origin}}</ref>

The Knights of Beauty source of power is unknown.<ref name="Hoyolab" /> 

==History==
The Knights of Beauty used to be a respected Faction that traveled the stars in the name of [[Beauty]].<ref>Simulated Universe, Curio: [[Simulated Universe/Curio#Robe of The Beauty|Robe of The Beauty]]</ref> However, sometime in the past, the [[Aeon]] [[Idrila]] fell and the Knights of Beauty disbanded.<ref>Loading Screen: [[Loading Screen#Factions|The Knights of Beauty]]</ref><ref>{{Ref/Readable|Glimpses into the Beyond}}</ref> While the Knights of Beauty still remained active, they began to roam around the cosmos as individuals rather than a group.<ref name="Argenti Mission">{{Ref/Mission|Night of Universal Hallucinations}}</ref> With the fall of Idrila, the Knights started to falter and many would lose their faith or honor.<ref name="Argenti 4" /> 

The [[Masked Fools]] would often amuse themselves with the Knights of Beauty while exploiting their faith for amusement.<ref>Simulated Universe, Curio: [[Simulated Universe/Curio#Spirit of the Knights of Beauty|Spirit of the Knights of Beauty]]</ref>

The Knights of Beauty now find themselves traveling the cosmos and helping those in need, regularly finding themselves in battles. Their exploits are not limited to combat, as many Knights would also assist others in improving their personal lives. The Knights of Beauty convinced a man only referred to as "Mr A" to stop throwing his trash on the street, and instead begin to clean the city. Another time, a Knight of Beauty helped create a culture of self-positivity among youths, teaching them that everyone has a unique brilliance.<ref name="Hoyolab" />

===Present===
When [[Pom-Pom]] decides to take the [[Astral Express]] to a Washtopia to clean the train up, the crew runs into [[Argenti]], a Knight of Beauty. He had just saved an [[Interastral Peace Corporation]] member, [[Velite]], from a cluster of [[The Swarm|Swarm]] units. Eventually, the group discovers that they were swallowed by a giant Swarm of unknown origin, but eventually break free when Argenti leaves the Express to fight the monster alone. He survives the battle.<ref name="Argenti Mission"/>

The Knights of Beauty were one of the several Factions invited to [[Penacony]] for the Charmony Festival, however they were yet to respond when the Astral Express arrived.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|The Youths Who Chase Dreams}}</ref> Eventually, Argenti arrives on Penacony (after some significant detours) and saves [[Aventurine]] from the Primordial Dreamscape. The [[Trailblazer]] and [[Firefly]] have a chance to run into him during the "Superstar Showdown" depending on the route they take. In the end they either convince him to stand down or defeat him, after which he wishes them luck on their mission.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Seabiscuit}}</ref>

Argenti later visited [[Kalevala]] and discovered the cursed sword [[Guyun]]. He pulled it out and heard its story to return to the Luofu. When he heard what would happen to the sword, he stole it, resulting in the Trailblazer and [[Yunli]] pursuing him. In the ensuing battle, Argenti used his own sword rather than Guyun and was defeated, asking Yunli to hear the sword out. Yunli, learning of the sword's past, decided to honor its last request and reforged it into a guzheng, while Argenti and [[Dahao]] scouted [[Aurum Alley]]. As punishment for "stealing" the sword, [[Huaiyan]] had him guard the guzheng and golden sapling and recite the story of the hero and sword to passers-by, which he gladly accepted as to spread the message of Beauty to the Xianzhou people. Once the punishment concluded, Argenti departed the Luofu.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Swords to Plowshares}}</ref>
