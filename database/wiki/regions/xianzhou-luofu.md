{{Lang|'''The Xianzhou Luofu'''|zh=仙舟「罗浮」|zh_rm=Xiānzhōu "Luófú"}} is the third accessible world in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]''. It can be accessed upon completing {{Mission|In the Sweltering Morning Sun}}.

It is one of the six flagships owned by the [[Hexafleet]] of the [[Xianzhou Alliance]]. It sails like a one-way arrow through the galaxy, with the goal of eradicating the [[Denizens of Abundance]]. The Luofu stands out among the other ships for its medical treatment and trade. People on the Luofu are considered laid-back compared to other ships.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|March to Mastery: A Star Is Born}}</ref>

==Geography==
[[File:Area Jade Gate.png|thumb|The Jade Gate as seen from [[Central Starskiff Haven]].]]
From the outside, the Luofu resembles an enormous [[starskiff]], its deck covered with several large, connected domes, each containing a different delve of the ship. Three-quarters of the way down the hull is a large ring which circles nearly the entire ship, save for a gap at the top. Floating between this gap is the Jade Gate, the main point of entry and exit for anyone visiting the massive ship.

The inside of the Luofu is composed of delves which total space exceeds the ship in size, made possible due to the Xianzhou's space-folding technology. These delves all vary in size and shape, all on a scale too large for any normal ship to contain. Some of the delves hold up entire residential and commercial districts, such as [[Exalting Sanctum]], while others contain vast luxuriant prairies like the Ever-Hunt Plains, which hosts fauna such as monkeys and fawn.

The Xianzhou rears several types of animals in the Ever-Hunt Plains, such as the Fungus Boar, [[Codex_of_Apocrypha#Beasts:_Yellow_Boulder|Yellow Boulder]], [[Tuskpir]], [[Codex_of_Apocrypha#Beasts:_Gentle_Halcyon|Gentle Halcyon]], and [[Codex_of_Apocrypha#Beasts:_Gentle_Halcyon|Downy Antler]]. Depth crawlers were a type of creature that was similar to [[void song whale]]s that once lived on the Luofu, but have been extinct for thousands of years.<ref>Visitor Dialogue: [[Yukong/Dialogue|Yukong]]</ref> While plants of the [[Tian Dong]] genus typically grow on land, Tian Dong plants of [[Scalegorge Waterscape]] stand out by extensively inhabiting the coastal areas of the Lunarescent Depths. Scalegorge alginate, Lunarescent Sea mint, and [[Dragon Scale Coral]] are also harvested there for medicinal purposes.<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Jiaoqiu's Consumable Prescription}}</ref>

Strange planets can be seen high in the sky, though some of them may be a projection of the delve's artificial sky dome. The [[Realm-Keeping Commission]]'s weather pavilion manages the environmental conditions on the Luofu, including the artificial wind, clouds, rain, and fog.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Devising Stratagems}}</ref> Heat is extracted from the core of the planet the ship is carrying to keep some of the delves warm.<ref>Central Starskiff Haven:[[Central_Starskiff_Haven#Investigations|Investigations]]</ref>

The visible space in the bustling skyport of [[Central Starskiff Haven]] is enough to fill up the entire Luofu. The bustling street of [[Starwatcher Avenue]] can be found here, where shops line the road for as far as the eye can see, and their lights are lit for perpetuity. The [[Sky-Faring Commission]]'s headquarters on the Luofu is the [[Palace of Astrum]], which also acts as the ship's bridge. [[Cloudford]] is the trade cargo port of the Loufu. With the arrival of numerous Outworlders, Starskiff Haven is alive with people from all walks of life. Goods from various worlds are delivered by transport starskiffs and unloaded at Cloudford, and every day it unceasingly handles huge amounts of port traffic.<ref>{{Ref/Readable|Travelogue on Xianzhou}}</ref><ref>NPC Dialogue: [[Ruoyue]]</ref>

Starskiffs on the Luofu are manufactured in [[Stargazer Navalia]]. [[Aurum Alley]] is the Exalting Sanctum's signature food street where tourists come to enjoy delicious food, from traditional dishes to roadside snacks, and experience street culture. The freight dock managed by the Aurum Alley Merchant Guild is mainly used for distributing commodities and logistics transportation. [[Fyxestroll Garden]] is garden delve created centuries ago by [[Foxian]] crafters temporarily occupied by the [[Ten-Lords Commission]] as the imprisonment site for [[heliobi]] that escaped from the [[Artisanship Commission (Location)|Artisanship Commission]]'s [[Creation Furnace]].

The [[Divination Commission (Location)|Divination Commission]] hosts the Luofu's [[Matrix of Prescience Ultima]]. [[Conclave Hall]] is the official location in the Divination Commission delve where issues are discussed publicly. The [[Seat of Divine Foresight]] is office of the [[Arbiter-General]] of the Luofu. The Luofu's [[Alchemy Commission (Location)|Alchemy Commission]] borders the [[Vidyadhara]] delve of Scalegorge Waterscape, which hosts the sealed entrance to the [[Ambrosial Arbor]], the Luofu's [[Plaguemark]]. The Alchemy Commission's medical texts are kept in the [[Elixir Research Terrace]], a building looking out over the sea. Medicine and traders flock to [[Healer's Market]]. In the past, only [[Elixir Seekers]] with permits could enter here to buy divine medicines. Healers gather to conduct medical research in the [[Medicine Bureau]]. The tree in its garden is one of the Ambrosial Arbor's roots.

A statue of [[Yubie]], the 91st incarnation of [[Imbibitor Lunae]], can be found at [[Dragonvista Rain Hall]], which seats the entrance to the [[Ancient Sea Palace Ruins]]. The enormous dragon palace of the Vidyadhara is buried in the seafloor valley, along with many Vidyadhara eggs preparing for hatching-rebirth. Hindered by the seal, the Ambrosial Arbor's roots curl into a dragon-shaped wood gall at the [[Palace Ruin Depths]], and no longer actively grow. Desalinated Lunarescent Seawater and [[Scalegorge Spring Water]] are potable. Beneath Scalegorge Waterscape is the entrance to the Luofu's [[The Shackling Prison|Shackling Prison]], which is spread out throughout multiple delves.<ref name ="rectitude">{{Ref/Mission|Inquisition to Rectitude}}</ref>

Aside from the aforementioned delves, there is also the Polar Delve and Cold Springs delve.<ref name ="rectitude" /><ref>{{Ref/Readable|Overview of Xianzhou's Materia Medica}}</ref>

===Etymology===
* The name Luofu {{Lang|zh=罗浮}} is likely derived from {{w|Mount Luofu}} {{Lang|zh=罗浮山|zh_rm=Luófú Shān}}, a sacred Taoist mountain located in Guangzhou, China. In Taoist tradition, Mount Luofu is renowned as the place where the Eastern Jin dynasty philosopher and alchemist {{w|Ge Hong}} {{Lang|zh=葛洪}} spent his later years refining elixirs in pursuit of immortality. The concept of ''{{w|Xian (Taoism)|xian}}'' {{Lang|zh=仙}}, meaning "immortal" or "transcendent being," is central to this tradition, and the same character ({{zh|仙}}) appears in Xianzhou {{Lang|zh=仙舟}}.
