'''Amphoreus''', also known as '''The Eternal Land''', is the fifth accessible world in ''[[Honkai: Star Rail]]''. It can be accessed upon completing {{Mission|A New Venture on the Eighth Dawn|showChapter=0}}.

It was an isolated sandbox world created by [[Scepter δ-me13]] under the administration of [[Lygus]], in a project known as [[The Amphoreus Experiment]]. The world was constructed using [[memoria]] and data,<ref name="H,HTCC">{{Ref/Mission|Hero, Honor That Crimson Call}}</ref> but following the events of {{Mission|As Tomorrow Became Yesterday}}, the Scepter was destroyed and Amphoreus is emerging from the [[Memory Zone]].<ref>{{Ref/Mission|As I've Written (Adventure Mission)}}</ref>

Amphoreus was a simulated world used as a sandbox by Scepter δ-me13,<ref name="H,HTCC" /> in its cultivation of the [[Destruction Equation]].<ref>As I've Written, ''δ-me13.exe'', [[As I've Written/δ-me13.exe#Scepter δ-me13|Experiment Fundamentals, Basic Info: Scepter δ-me13]]</ref> The make-up of Amphoreus exists purely through data and memoria,<ref name="H,HTCC" /> with the core factors operating as electrical signals.<ref name="12factors">As I've Written, ''δ-me13.exe'', [[As I've Written/δ-me13.exe#Twelve Factors|Experiment Fundamentals, Algorithmic Logic: Twelve Factors]]</ref> Whenever Amphoreus would be extrapolated by the Scepter, a full life-cycle of the world would be initiated, with the Scepter loading in electrical signals and factors to collect data.

===Pre-Main Story===
Prior to the [[Astral Express]]' arrival into Amphoreus, The Amphoreus Experiment had progressed to its last stage of the experiment. This stage, internally known as "Era Nova," involved twelve factors being loaded into Amphoreus in the form of [[Titans]] and [[Coreflame]]s with associated [[Chrysos Heir]]s, being tasked to retrieve the Coreflame from its Titan, in an attempt to save the world from the [[black tide]].<ref name="12factors" /> The Black Tide being a form of the Destruction Equation.<ref>As I've Written, ''δ-me13.exe'', [[As I've Written/δ-me13.exe#Black Tide|Experiment Progress: Era Nova, Base Frame Structure: Black Tide]]</ref> At this point, the scepter had extrapolated at least 28,371,292 cycles of Amphoreus, across the experiment.<ref>As I've Written, ''δ-me13.exe'', [[As I've Written/δ-me13.exe#Electrical Signal Sequence (Iterated)|Experiment Progress: Era Nova, Electrical Signal Sequence (Iterated)]]</ref> Progress was stopped however, due the combined efforts of [[Phainon]] and [[Cyrene]], two Chrysos Heirs who were able to stall the system, forcing the cycle to be repeatedly extrapolated through the repeated killing of [[Cyrene]]'s signal.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|Hero, Ignite That Primal Sun}}</ref>

===Post Main Story===
Following the events of [[As Tomorrow Became Yesterday]], Scepter δ-me13 was destroyed, with it's data recovered by [[The Herta|Herta]] and [[Screwllum]], while the electrical signals of Amphoreus were safely placed in [[As I've Written]] by the [[Demiurge]]. Currently, Amphoreus is being reborn within a [[Memory Zone]] through the [[Remembrance]]<ref>Parlor Car, Interactable: [[Parlor Car#IPC Broadcast #6|IPC Broadcast #6]]</ref> caused by the Demiurge, left in the wake of Scepter δ-me13's destruction. It is unpredictable when or if Amphoreus will return, but Herta claims spreading the story of Amphoreus will quicken its creation, and Amphoreus should return to what we once knew.<ref>{{Ref/Mission|As I've Written (Adventure Mission)}}</ref>

===Etymology===
* As confirmed by the developers, Amphoreus refers to a type of container used in Ancient Greek vase paintings, the {{Lang|{{w|amphora}}|el=ἀμφορεύς|el_rm=amphora}}.<ref name="VA">{{Ref/YouTube|Visit Amphoreus, a place even the Aeon of the Trailblaze hasn't set foot in!|date=Jan 05 2025}}</ref>
** In the myth of {{W|Pandora's box}}, the box is said to be an amphora, a connection the developers are aware of.<ref name="VA" />
* The world's Chinese name, {{Lang|zh=翁法罗斯|zh_rm=Wēngfǎluósī}}, means {{w|omphalos}}, a rounded stone representing the "navel" of the earth in ancient {{w|Greek mythology}}. The Japanese text transliterates this identically as {{ja|オンパロス|rm=Onparosu}}, but every other transliteration follows the name Amphoreus.
** In ancient Greece, the omphalos stone at {{w|Delphi}} was considered the "navel of the world" and was believed to have marked the center of the earth.
** In Greek mythology, the same term also referred to the boulder that the Greek goddess {{w|Rhea (mythology)|Rhea}} made the Titan {{w|Cronus}} swallow instead of their son {{w|Zeus}}, which Cronus vomited out when Zeus tried to free his brothers who had been swallowed by their father.
* "The Eternal Land" may refer to the nickname for the city of {{w|Rome}}, "Urbs Aeterna," meaning "The Eternal City."
