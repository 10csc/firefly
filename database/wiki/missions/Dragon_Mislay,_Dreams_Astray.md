'''''Dragon Mislay, Dreams Astray''''' is a sub-mission of {{Mission|Desolate Depths of Despair}}. It starts after the [[Trailblazer]] speaks with [[Fu Xuan]] and takes a starskiff to [[Scalegorge Waterscape]].

==Steps==
# Leave the [[Healer's Market]] on your own
#* (Optional) Talk with people in the market to understand what is happening
# Find out where the Trailblazer and the others are
# Take the starskiff and enter Scalegorge Waterscape

==Gameplay Notes==
* This sub-mission is considered a challenge, and pressing Esc offers the option to "Pause and Save" or "Quit Completely" to exit.
** If "Quit Completely" is selected, the sub-mission may be restarted after watching the previous cutscene again.
* This mission features several Trial and Story characters:
** [[Dan Heng]] [Trial]
** [[Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae]] [Story]
** [[Blade]] [Story], not controllable by the player
