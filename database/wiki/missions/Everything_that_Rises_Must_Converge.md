'''''Everything that Rises Must Converge''''' is the ninth part of the [[Trailblaze Mission]] chapter [[In Our Time]]. It automatically begins after completing ''[[Beauty and Destruction]]''.

==Steps==
# Speak with your companions and sort the information you have
# Search the Grand Theater
# Follow the guide through the ascending corridor
# Watch Act I
# Finish Act I
# Follow the guide to watch Act II
# Finish Act II
# Advance through Act III and cooperate to complete the performance
# Take to the stage for the fated battle
